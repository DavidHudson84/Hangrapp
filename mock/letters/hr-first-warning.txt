Dear Marco,

This letter confirms the outcome of our meeting on 10 June 2026 and is a first written warning about the completion of the daily safety device tests on the press line.

The issue is this. Every morning, before the press line is used, the two-hand controls and the guard interlocks on the LP-580 shirt unit, the UP-120 utility press and both trouser machines have to be function-tested and the result written on the weekly test record. That takes about four minutes. It is on the daily start-up checklist and it is a requirement of the manufacturer's own manual, which we hold on file.

On 18 May, 26 May, 2 June and 8 June the test record was left blank for the whole line. On 8 June I watched you start the utility press and begin work without testing anything. When I raised it you said the machines had been fine the day before.

We had talked about this twice already — on 21 April at the toolbox meeting, where I went through the record with the whole plant, and again on 12 May one to one, when you told me you would pick it up. I am putting it in writing now because two conversations have not changed it.

I want to be plain about why this matters rather than just tell you it is a rule. A two-hand control is what stops a press closing on a hand. It fails silently — nothing looks different until the day it does not stop, and by then somebody is in the Bendigo Base emergency department. The test is not paperwork. The record is what proves the test happened.

At the meeting you said you had been under pressure with the volume in May and that the test felt like a delay when there were racks waiting. I accept the volume was heavy. It is not a reason to skip it, and if the workload is genuinely making the start-up impossible then that is something to raise with Sharon or with me so we can move the start time, not something to solve by leaving it out.

What has to change: from Monday 15 June 2026, the daily safety device test is completed on every machine on the press line before any garment is pressed, and the weekly test record is signed each morning. Sharon will check the record daily for the next four weeks and then weekly.

If the tests and the record are not completed as required, further disciplinary action may follow, which could include a final written warning or termination of your employment.

This warning will stay on your file. You were offered a support person for the meeting on 10 June and chose to attend on your own. If you disagree with anything in this letter, put it in writing within seven days and it will be placed on your file alongside it.

Yours sincerely,
