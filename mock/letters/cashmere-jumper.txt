DLI TABS — Stretch, Shrinkage & Distortion sets out why: felting in an animal fibre is the mechanical interlocking of the scale structure under heat and agitation, and it is permanent. There is no process that reverses it. Blocking it out would tear the fabric before it moved the measurements.
