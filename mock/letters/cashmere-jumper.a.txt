The cause is settled by the log. Cashmere felts when heat, moisture and mechanical action let the scale structure on the fibre interlock, and it is irreversible once it happens — the fibres are physically hooked together, not merely compressed. Ten degrees and a general extract instead of a reduced one is precisely the margin between a safe wool cycle and a felted one. DLI TABS — Stretch, Shrinkage & Distortion covers the mechanism and confirms there is no recovery process: blocking a felted knit out to measurement tears it before it moves.

The method choice was right, which is worth saying to him. The label authorised professional wet cleaning and we had wet cleaned the same jumper in September 2025 and again in March 2026 without a mark on it. The failure was the machine parameter, not the decision. Say that the machine has been re-parameterised and the wool settings locked at the panel — a customer who hears that a fault was found and closed usually stops looking for an argument.
