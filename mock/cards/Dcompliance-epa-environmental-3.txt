Third part of the Main Street Dry Cleaners environmental compliance manual: waste collections, trade waste, air monitoring and spill response.
Notification triggers and timeframes:
Pollution incident causing or threatening material harm - EPA hotline 1300 372 842, as soon as practicable after becoming aware.
Solvent, separator water or any prohibited substance entering the sewer - Coliban Water 1300 363 200, immediately and within 2 hours.
Spill to soil, stormwater, the laneway or beyond the boundary - EPA and City of Greater Bendigo, same day.
Death, serious injury or exposure needing medical treatment - WorkSafe 13 23 60, immediately, site preserved.
Fire, unknown or continuing release, or anyone overcome - 000 immediately.
Any incident - Karen Whitfield immediately, broker Hargreaves & Locke within 48 hours, Sovereign Environmental same day if waste is involved, Castleton Property Group if the building is affected. Where in doubt, notify.
Spill response order is people, containment, clean-up, records. Over 5 L or outside a bund: evacuate, cover drains before absorbing, no water, consign as N220 Category B. A spill reaching a drain, the laneway or soil is serious whatever the volume and is never flushed.
Separator water is never discharged to sewer in any volume.
Trade waste agreement TW-4471 with Coliban Water: 4.5 kL daily; 1.5 L/s; pH 6.0 to 10.0; 38 C at the boundary; suspended solids 600 mg/L; BOD 600 mg/L; oil and grease 100 mg/L; chlorinated hydrocarbons not detectable; trading hours only. NATA sample each March.
Monitoring: weekly leak and separator checks, monthly PID survey at six fixed points, 10 ppm action level, annual ventilation check. Collections quarterly by Sovereign, second week of February, May, August and November. Waste records kept 7 years.
