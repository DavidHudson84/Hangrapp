The Bendigo lease, third of three parts: assignment, default, security, make good and Retail Leases Act provisions.
Assignment needs consent, refusable only on section 60 grounds; consent is deemed if the Landlord does not answer in 28 days. A change of majority shareholding counts.
Default: rent unpaid 14 days, a breach unremedied after a section 146 notice, insolvency, abandonment.
Essential terms include rent, outgoings, use, solvent storage (7.1), EPA duties (7.3), the boiler (7.5), trade waste (7.6), insurance; breach is repudiation.
Security: the bank guarantee must expire no earlier than 6 months after the Terminating date, be replaced with three months' reviewed rent plus GST within 28 days of a review on request, and restored in 14 days if called on. Returned 30 days after vacant possession, the exit report and payment.
Guarantor Karen Louise Whitfield is liable as principal debtor through any further term.
Make good: vacant possession; all Tenant's plant out; services capped; every tank, pipe, still, separator and duct drained, purged and certified free of solvent; the vent stack and boiler flue removed and the penetrations made good; plinths out and the slab reinstated. Not complete until the exit report is given and any remediation done. The Landlord may elect, three months out, to keep an item of plant at no cost.
Land tax: clause 15.5 says nothing in the lease requires the Tenant to pay land tax or any amount on account of it, and any such term is void under section 50.
Landlord's lease preparation costs are not recoverable (section 51). Disputes go to the Small Business Commission for mediation.
Executed 14 February 2024. Annexures: the plan; the Terrafield baseline report TF-2401-118HG of 22 February 2024; Landlord's fixtures; Tenant's plant list.
