Final part of the Fulton FB-030 manual, Rev 7 (March 2019): fault codes, troubleshooting, spare parts, warranty and record sheets. Serial FB030-14-0873, plant registration VIC-PV-118-4470, expiry 30 June 2027.
Vantage 3 codes run B01 to B38, with B14, B19 and B37 reserved and unused; a reserved code on the display means a controller fault, B38.
Reset classes: A operator resettable once after the listed check, and Class B if it returns; B technician only, a safety device lockout or a repeat of a Class A code; C licensed Type B gasfitter only, the gas train and valve proving lockouts.
An operator may never reset a Class B or C code. B06, B16, B25, B26, B27, B28, B29 and B33 are lockouts on devices that exist solely to prevent an explosion or a shell failure; repeated resetting of one is the most dangerous act an operator can commit.
B05, B06, B16, B25, B26, B27, B30, B31, B32 and B33 are reported to the person with management or control the day they occur; every lockout is logged.
Parts: FB-1010 handhole gasket, never reused; FB-1024 gauge glass 24 months; FB-1030 probe assembly 48 months; FB-1040 relief valve, set 690 kPa, overhaul 24 months; FB-1051 electrode and FB-1059 gas filter element 12 months. Never fit a shelf spare relief valve; it must be set, tested and sealed by an accredited organisation.
Warranty: pressure vessel 60 months, burner, gas train, controls, pump and ancillaries 24 months, from commissioning, conditional on the six-monthly burner service, annual AS 2593 function test, AS 3788 inspections, valve overhaul and water test records. Voided outright by a defective, bypassed or overdue safety device, breaking the valve seal, raising the setpoint above 620 kPa or the high limit above 655 kPa, disabling valve proving, or a lapsed plant registration.
