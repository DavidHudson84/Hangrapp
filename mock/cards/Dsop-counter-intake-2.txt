SOP 01 Counter Intake, second of three parts — stains, care labels, the at-risk list and the authorisation.
MSDC-SOP-01, version 4, effective 1 March 2026.
Stains are written as location, size, colour and likely class — tannin, oily, protein, dye, invisible, combination or unknown. Invisible sugar staining browns after heat — ask if it has had a drink on it.
Never promise removal of any mark; write "Advised mark may not lift" on the docket.
A label that does not account for trim is non-compliant with AS/NZS 1957 and cannot be relied on.
Circle symbols: P = perchloroethylene and everything permitted under F, the Union XL-800. F = hydrocarbon only, perc not permitted, the Realstar HM-450. W = wet cleaning, Speed Queen. Iron dots 110 °C, 150 °C, 200 °C. A bar means gentler, a cross means never, F never means P — an F garment run in perc cost $640 in 2025.
No label, cut out or illegible is at-risk. Two labels that disagree: follow the more restrictive. Believe the fabric over the label.
At-risk under clause 7: beading and sequins; prints and foils; PU, PVC and coated fabrics; fused constructions; leather and suede trim; faux fur; unlabelled garments; anything over five years old; wedding gowns and formalwear; curtains and household textiles, needing a drop measurement and Form MSDC-F04; anything already damaged or said to be precious; self-belts and detachable trims, which come off at intake and are docketed separately; collars with product build-up, routed to hydrocarbon or pre-spotted and never sent to perc untreated; unstable dyes.
Form MSDC-F02 is mandatory for any clause 7 garment, a stated value over $500, any send-out, anything irreplaceable, an absent or illegible label, and any mark advised as unlikely to lift. "Damage may occur" is not a risk statement.
