The Sankosha press line manual, second of two parts — the finishing quality fault table, the fault codes, the maintenance schedule, consumables and warranty for the LP-580, UP-120, TT-240 and TL-260. Rev 5, March 2022.
Sections here: 6.5 to 6.8 shine and pad changes; 7 topper and legger, with nine trouser finishing faults at 7.7; 8 finishing quality faults; 9 consumables; 10 maintenance; 11 fault codes; 12 warranty; Appendices A to D.
Section 8 is what to use when a garment comes back — cause, prevention and whether it is recoverable. Faults: shine, seam impression, double crease, water spotting, scorch, flattened nap, pressed-in stain, collar puckering, colour change under heat, glazing at the crease, damp garment at the hanger, cracked or melted buttons, lining pulled and rippled, and shrinkage on wool. Scorch, colour change, shrinkage and melted buttons are not recoverable; pressed-in stain rarely is.
Fault codes: SU-01 to SU-18 (LP-580), UP-01 to UP-10 (UP-120), TT-01 to TT-09 (TT-240), TL-01 to TL-09 (TL-260), each marked technician or operator; 11.5 troubleshoots the eight commonest.
Service intervals: daily and weekly by the operator, monthly and above by supervisor or technician. Monthly two-hand control, interlock, light curtain, E-stop and manual release tests; quarterly steam trap test; annual electrical test and full safety certification.
Warranty 24 months from commissioning, 60 months on steam-side castings; commissioned 12 February 2018 and 4 November 2019, all cover now expired.
Guards, two-hand controls and E-stops must be certified annually by an authorised technician; without a current certificate a machine is not to be operated. Defeating a two-hand control or interlock, or altering the 500 ms timing window, voids both warranty and certificate.
