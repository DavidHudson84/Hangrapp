Fourth and final part of the Main Street Dry Cleaners environmental compliance manual: incident records, record retention, audits, training and the compliance calendar. Approved by Karen Whitfield, Director, 1 March 2026, acknowledged by Peter Nguyen as plant duty holder.
Every spill and near miss is recorded on the Appendix A form within 24 hours and signed by Karen Whitfield. A form giving "operator error" as the cause is returned unsigned.
Record retention: solvent transfer log, bund register, PID survey, waste collection log and tracking records, trade waste results, 7 years; spill kit, leak and separator checks 3 years; incident reports, health monitoring and SDS 30 years; change reviews and audit reports permanent.
The internal audit runs each March, clause by clause, re-testing the permission tier, the dangerous goods thresholds, the waste characterisation and the trade waste agreement.
External reviews: Calder Environmental 11 May 2022, 6 findings; an unannounced EPA visit 14 September 2023 after an odour complaint, no further action; Calder 3 March 2024, 2 findings; Calder 9 March 2026, 3 findings, 1 still open on the six-point PID survey. Next review March 2027.
Compliance calendar: waste collections in week 2 of February, May, August and November; internal audit and trade waste sample in March; boiler tune in April; Sovereign's permission and insurance evidence in May; health monitoring offers in June; manual review in July; PPE audit in October; shutdown in December. Monthly: bund inspection, spill kit check, PID survey, grille check. Weekly: leak and separator check.
Refresher training each February for the plant operator, presser, driver and owner; every two years for counter and office staff.
