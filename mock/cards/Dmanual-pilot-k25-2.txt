Fault code, maintenance, parts and air quality half of the Pilot K25 air compressor manual, Rev 3, second of two parts — Sections 6.5 to 13 and the appendices.
Pilot K25, serial PK25-19-0883, Bendigo. Quote the serial with every order.
Fault codes A01 to A34. S codes shut the machine down and need ENTER to acknowledge; W codes are warnings only.
A01 discharge above 110 °C trip, A02 above 105 °C warning, A16 separator differential above 0.8 bar, A17 above 1.2 bar (a garment risk), A18 oil low, A19 to A23 service due (oil filter, air filter, oil, separator, major), A28 to A30 drain faults, A32 dew point above 12 °C, A33 above 15 °C. While A33 stands, do not put air on garments.
Oil is due at 2,000 running hours or 12 months — P15 and P16 were cut from their 4,000 h defaults and must not be put back — and is changed at the 2,000 h or 6-month service. Refill 16 L Pilot SS-8000, PK-OIL-8000-20; separator lid bolts 32 Nm, sump plugs 45 Nm.
Schedule: daily; weekly; 500 h or 1 month; 2,000 h or 6 months — oil filter PK-OF-2140, air filter PK-AF-3355, separator PK-SE-4720, scavenge kit PK-SCV-08, filters PK-LF-030P and PK-LF-030C; 4,000 h or 12 months, technician — carbon PK-LF-030A, belts PK-BS-1150, drain kits PK-ZD-12K, oil sample PK-OSK-01; 8,000 h or 24 months — safety valve PK-SV-105, thermostatic element PK-TV-71. Annual AS 3788 vessel inspection by Goldfields Plant Inspection Services; internal every four years.
Load 7.0 bar (P02), unload 8.0 bar (P01). Do not raise them — each 1 bar costs about 7% energy; presses need 6.2 bar.
Warranty 24 months or 6,000 hours, airend 60 months or 20,000 hours; claims in writing within 14 days. Void by non-genuine separator or filters, altering P03 or P04, breaking the safety valve seal, or welding to the receiver.
