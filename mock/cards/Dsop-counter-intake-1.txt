SOP 01 Counter Intake, Inspection and At-Risk Tagging, first of three parts — from the greeting to what must be written on the docket.
MSDC-SOP-01, version 4, effective 1 March 2026. Owner Karen Whitfield. Review due March 2027.
Applies at Shop 4, 118 Hargreaves Street Bendigo and Shop 12, Lansell Square, Kangaroo Flat.
Docket prefixes: Bendigo B-, Kangaroo Flat K-. The app issues the number at the site of intake — never handwritten, never reused, never edited once the customer has left. A K- docket keeps its K- number through the Bendigo plant and on every later record.
Pieces are docket/piece: B-24118/1 jacket, /2 trousers, /3 self-belt.
Every docket records name, phone, site, the staff member who inspected (not who took payment), pieces counted twice out loud with the customer, article, service, condition notes, at-risk flag, authorisation number, photographs and a collection date.
Turnaround five working days at both sites; specialist send-outs three to four weeks; shirts booked before 09:00 at Bendigo are next-day; a K- docket raised after 10:00 loses a day on the van run.
No phone number: for ordinary work write "no contact number given" and book it in. For anything at-risk the rule is no number, no garment.
Inspection order, every garment, every time: collar, shoulders, sleeves and cuffs, front, back, hem, lining, pockets, closures, trims. Daylight only — the 6,500 K bar at Bendigo, the handheld torch at Kangaroo Flat.
Targets: shirt 20 seconds, trousers or plain dress 60 seconds, jacket or suit 90 seconds, at-risk 3 to 5 minutes, curtains 3 minutes per pair.
Every note gives where, what and how big in millimetres.
Every pocket emptied by hand; "pockets emptied" written on every docket, nil included. One lip balm through a perc load cost $780 in 2024.
