The Trevil finishing room manual, first of two parts, covering front matter, safety, services, specifications and the operation of the two finishers as far as 6.7.
Rev 3, September 2020, superseding Rev 2 of March 2017.
Installed at Main Street Dry Cleaners, Shop 4, 118 Hargreaves Street, Bendigo VIC 3550.
Machines: Diamant SA-90 steam-air cabinet finisher, DSA90-2017-0231; Princess FF-300 form finisher or Suzy, PFF300-2019-0884; Spotmaster SB-40 vacuum spotting board, SSB40-2016-0512.
Sections here: 1 front matter, 2 safety, 3 services, 4 specifications, 5 SA-90, 6.1-6.7 FF-300.
Not in this part, all in part 2: Section 7 the spotting board with its chemistry set, stain table and fibre cautions, and Sections 8 maintenance, 9 fault codes, 10 parts, 11 warranty.
Safety covers the whole room, spotting board included: never mix oxygen bleach with an acid formula, rust remover with an alkali, or amyl acetate with an oxidiser; amyl acetate TVC-05 is flammable, flash point about 25 C, and is used only under the running hood; no chemical gun or solvent work with the extraction fan off.
Hood face velocity at least 0.5 m/s, measured quarterly; room ventilation 8 air changes per hour. The SB-40 hood must not be joined into a common duct with the finisher vents.
SB-40 supply must be RCD-protected at 30 mA, tested monthly. Eyewash within 10 seconds of the board. Poisons Information Centre 13 11 26. Cool a burn or flush an eye for 20 minutes, skin for 15.
Steam up to 690 kPa, not below 400 kPa at the header. Over-temperature cut-outs: SA-90 145 C, FF-300 135 C.
SA-90 has 24 programme memories and P1 to P14, FF-300 20 memories and F1 to F12, 12 factory programmes each. P14, the door interlock, must never be set to 0.
Fault codes are not tabled here; the prefixes are SA, FF and SB.
