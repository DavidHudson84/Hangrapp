The Kangaroo Flat licence, third of three parts: default and termination, make good, the Retail Leases Act position and security.
Default: any fee unpaid 7 days, a breach unremedied 14 days after notice, insolvency, 7 days closed without consent, a false turnover statement, or no replacement guarantor within 30 days.
Essential terms: the fee, turnover reporting, trading, use and no-plant clause 7.2, insurance, assignment.
The Licensor may terminate at once, without notice to remedy, for a breach of clause 7.2, a safety risk or insolvency.
The Licensee may terminate on 6 months' notice given any time after 30 June 2026, paying two months' licence fee and promotion levy when it takes effect.
Make good: remove garments, stock, counter, racking, signage and fittings; fascia to a paint-ready finish; floor back to base building standard; the hand basin and its water capped by a licensed plumber.
Every customer garment must go back first. Failing to vacate costs double the daily licence fee; no compensation for goodwill or fitout value.
The Licensor's position is that the Act does not apply. If it does, the Licensee is not liable for land tax or any amount on account of land tax, relocation runs subject to section 39, and the Licensor's document costs are not recoverable.
A disclosure statement and a copy of the agreement were given on 24 May 2024, without admitting the Act applies.
Security: bank guarantee for the Item 20 amount, expiring no earlier than 6 months after the Expiry date, restored in 14 days if called on, returned 30 days after vacating and make good. Guarantor Karen Louise Whitfield is liable as principal debtor.
Executed 11 June 2024. Annexures: the plan of the 24 m2 area, 6.0 m by 4.0 m, racking for 220 garments; the centre rules; the monthly turnover form.
