The Bendigo lease, second of three parts: repair, environmental and plant obligations, insurance.
Repair: Tenant keeps the Premises in good repair, fair wear and tear excepted, including the trade waste pit, separator and flue.
Solvent store, clause 7.1: all solvent, waste solvent and residue in the bunded store at the rear of the plant area; bund impervious, free of stored water, holding 110% of the largest container and not less than 220 L; at most two 200 L drums of unused solvent and two of waste residue on site; drums closed, labelled, on a sealed chemically resistant surface not draining to stormwater; manifest and safety data sheets kept at the Premises and at the Bath Lane entry; store locked when unattended.
Spill kits at the store and machines; spills cleaned at once; Landlord told in 24 hours of a spill over 5 L or one reaching soil or a drain.
EPA: Tenant holds the general environmental duty, moves prescribed industrial waste only through an authorised transporter, keeps certificates five years, and passes on regulator notices in 3 business days.
Contamination indemnity survives the lease, excluding the Item 27 baseline unless the Tenant's use made it worse.
Boiler registration VIC-PV-118-4470 and the air receiver registered to AS 3788, inspected yearly.
Trade waste: separator emptied as prescribed industrial waste every six months; nothing solvent-bearing to sewer or stormwater.
Carbon adsorber and stack run so the perchloroethylene exposure standard is not exceeded; air monitored yearly.
Exit site condition report due in the last three months of the Term, at the Tenant's cost, to the Annexure B scope; remediation to baseline.
Tenant insurance: liability, pollution, plate glass, plant, customers' goods, machinery breakdown, workers compensation.
