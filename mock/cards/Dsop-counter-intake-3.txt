SOP 01 Counter Intake, third of three parts — signing the authorisation, photographs, declining, handover to the plant and the Kangaroo Flat drop-store rule.
MSDC-SOP-01, version 4, effective 1 March 2026. Review due March 2027.
The customer signs MSDC-F02 before the garment is accepted — never at collection. One signed at collection on a beaded jacket cost $1,150 in 2022. The form does not exclude the consumer guarantees that cannot be excluded. It is scanned to the app before the garment leaves the counter and kept seven years.
Photographs are mandatory for every at-risk garment, every F02, every household textile job, every concern raised, every send-out and every decline. Site tablet only, never a personal phone. Minimum six shots, eight to ten for leather, gowns and curtains: whole front, whole back, the care label, each defect, each at-risk feature and the docket number. Yellow 150 mm scale card beside anything close. Kept seven years and never edited or cropped.
Decline in four situations only: no safe method identifiable; the garment will not survive the process; soiling we are not equipped for; the expectation cannot be met.
Route tabs: no tab perc, Union XL-800; green hydrocarbon, Realstar HM-450; blue wet clean, Speed Queen; red at-risk, do not run without reading the authorisation; white send-out. A red tab always travels with a green or a blue.
If the plant disagrees, the operator stops, the garment goes on the query rail, Sharon rules the same day, Karen decides a deadlock, and the customer is rung before anything runs.
Kangaroo Flat, working alone: never accept an at-risk garment on your own judgement. Photograph it, ring Bendigo on (03) 5443 8817 for Sharon or Karen, then do what you are told. If nobody answers, the garment is not accepted.
