The Kangaroo Flat drop store licence, first of three parts: the schedule, the nature of the licence, term, fee and turnover.
Licensor Aurora Retail Management Pty Ltd ACN 132 884 019, manager and agent for the owner of Lansell Square.
Licensee Aveline Holdings Pty Ltd ACN 617 903 558 trading as Main Street Dry Cleaners. Guarantor Karen Louise Whitfield.
Licensed Area Shop 12, Lansell Square, 233 High Street, Kangaroo Flat VIC 3555 - 24 m2 on the mall alignment.
A licence, not a lease: no exclusive possession, no estate in land, and the Licensor may relocate under clause 8.6.
Agreement dated 11 June 2024. Fitout period 17 to 30 June 2024, no fee payable.
Term three years, 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2027. No option to renew.
To negotiate a further licence the Licensee must give written notice by 31 December 2026; the Licensor answers within 60 days, and neither side must agree.
Licence fee $21,600.00 plus GST a year, paid $1,800.00 plus GST monthly in advance. The fee is fixed for the Term - there is no review.
Promotion levy $1,296.00 plus GST a year, $108.00 plus GST monthly, raisable once an accounting year by the greater of 5% or Melbourne CPI on one month's notice.
Turnover fee 4% of gross turnover above the $180,000.00 threshold, plus GST, due 30 days after the Licensor's account.
Outgoings are included in the licence fee; there is no separate contribution. The Licensee pays its own electricity, telephone and merchant fees.
Security: bank guarantee $5,940.00, three months' fee plus GST.
Insurance: public liability $20,000,000; customers' goods at least $50,000.
Approved trading hours Monday to Friday 9:00 am to 5:00 pm and Saturday 9:00 am to 1:00 pm.
Turnover reported monthly by the 7th, with a certified annual statement within 60 days of 30 June.
