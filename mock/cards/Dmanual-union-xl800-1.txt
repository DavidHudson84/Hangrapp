Operations and maintenance manual for the Union XL-800 dry cleaning machine, first of two parts: safety, specifications, installation, machine description, control panel, programmes and daily operation to the running of a cycle — Sections 1 to 8.4.
Enclosed three-tank perchloroethylene dry-to-dry machine, 25 kg nominal load, with distillation still and refrigerated solvent recovery.
Serial UXL8-2019-0447, built 2018, commissioned 14 March 2019 at Main Street Dry Cleaners, Shop 4, 118 Hargreaves Street, Bendigo VIC 3550.
Manual Rev 4, January 2024, superseding Rev 3, June 2021.
The fault code table is Section 11, in the other part.
Solvent perchloroethylene, UN 1897, CAS 127-18-4. Working charge 380 L. Tank 1 clean distilled 120 L, Tank 2 working 130 L, Tank 3 filtered 130 L. Still batch 75 L at 22 L/h. Carbon adsorber 45 kg, desorbed every 60 cycles.
Exposure standard 50 ppm 8-hour TWA, 150 ppm 15-minute STEL.
Supply 400 V 3-phase, 32 A full load, 40 A C curve protection. Steam 550 to 620 kPa; still jacket design 700 kPa, relief valve set 650 kPa. Refrigerant R404A, 4.3 kg.
Filter clean differential 30 to 60 kPa, warning 180 kPa, trip 220 kPa. Still overtemperature trip 138 °C. Filter service alarm 250 hours, still service alarm 500 hours.
Factory programmes P01 to P12, custom P13 to P20. Supervisor code default 2140, to be changed at commissioning.
Safety rules that must not be broken: isolate all four energy sources — electrical, steam, compressed air, solvent under head — before work inside a guard or panel; the still and the base tank are confined spaces under AS 2865, entry only on permit, jacket below 40 °C, 30 minutes purge, oxygen 19.5 to 23.5%, perc below 25 ppm; never defeat the door, button trap, lint trap or still access interlocks.
