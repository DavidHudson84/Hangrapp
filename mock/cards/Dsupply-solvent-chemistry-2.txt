Second part of the Solvex supply agreement, from the waste transport certificates through payment, termination and the annexures.
Supplier Solvex Chemicals Australia Pty Ltd ABN 88 104 552 719, Campbellfield. Customer Aveline Holdings Pty Ltd ACN 617 903 558 trading as Main Street Dry Cleaners, Bendigo.
Collections are quarterly, in the weeks commencing the second Monday of March, June, September and December, 7:00 to 12:00 from the rear laneway off Bath Lane.
An on-call collection must be requested where more than four 200 L drums are held; the supplier attends within five business days.
Charges excluding GST: scheduled collection up to 4 x 200 L drums $420.00; each additional 200 L drum $96.00; each 20 L pail $38.00; on-call collection $610.00; recovery drum $88.00; rejected container $145.00; certificate administration $46.00 per load.
Transporter Sovereign Environmental Services Pty Ltd, EPA permissioned. The customer confirms the generator section of each Waste Transport Certificate before the load leaves site.
Duty of care: the customer is the generator, owes the general environmental duty under the Environment Protection Act 2017 (Vic), and those obligations are not transferred by this agreement. Classifying, describing, storing lawfully and reporting spills stay with the customer.
Customer keeps waste records three years; the supplier keeps its copies seven years.
Payment is due thirty days from the end of the month of invoice. Interest 1.25% per month overdue, and supply may be suspended once an amount is more than thirty days overdue.
Either party may terminate on not less than sixty days' written notice, or immediately for an unremedied material breach after fourteen days.
