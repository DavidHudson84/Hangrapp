Tenant Aveline Holdings Pty Ltd ACN 617 903 558 trading as Main Street Dry Cleaners. Guarantor Karen Louise Whitfield.

Retail Leases Act 2003 (Vic) lease dated 14 February 2024.
Term five years, 1 March 2024 to 28 February 2029. Further term of five years, 1 March 2029 to 28 February 2034.
Option exercisable by written notice not earlier than 1 March 2028 and not later than 31 August 2028, with the Landlord's section 28 notice due 6 to 12 months before that day.

Recoverable outgoings limited to clause 5.2 - rates, water, fire services property levy, building insurance, common area costs, waste, management fee capped at 4%.
Clause 5.6 bars land tax or any amount on account of it (section 50), capital costs, and anything not listed or disclosed.
