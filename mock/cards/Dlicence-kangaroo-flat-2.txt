The Kangaroo Flat licence, second of three parts: turnover penalties, trading, use, relocation, insurance, assignment.
No plant and no cleaning on site, clause 7.2: no dry cleaning or washing machine, dryer, boiler, still, steam generator, compressor or press; no cleaning, laundering or bulk pressing; no solvent of any kind, including perchloroethylene and hydrocarbon, and no solvent-contaminated waste, filter or residue; no water beyond the hand basin; no process making steam, odour, heat, effluent or trade waste. Only a hand-held garment steamer of up to 2,000 W is allowed.
Garments must be cleaned at the Bendigo plant or another licensed plant, carried in covered vehicles, taken back if uncollected for 60 days, and must not give off solvent odour.
Trading: open through approved hours, counter attended; liquidated damages $150.00 plus GST for each day not open; up to 10 extra Christmas days on 21 days' notice.
A late monthly statement costs $50.00 plus GST a week to a $500.00 cap; over 30 days late, turnover may be assessed at 110% of the best of the last 12 months.
Audit once an accounting year and once within 12 months after the Term; the Licensee pays if turnover was understated by over 5%. Fitout to approved drawings, one approved fascia sign.
Relocation on 3 months' notice to a comparable area, moving costs reimbursed to the Item 27 cap; the Licensee may instead terminate by notice within 21 days.
Insurance: public and products liability at the Item 21 amount, customers' goods at the Item 22 amount, fittings and stock at full value, plate glass, workers compensation.
The licence is personal: no assignment, sublicence or sharing, and a change of majority control needs consent. On a sale of the business the Licensor must not unreasonably refuse a new licence.
