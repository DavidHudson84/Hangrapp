The Meridian business pack wording, second of four parts: the property exclusions, then Sections 2 and 3 and the first half of the bailee's section.
Policy number, insured, period of cover and premium are not stated in this part — see part 1.
Section 4 bailee's / customers' goods limits: $50,000 any one event; $25,000 any one garment, matched suit or matched set; $20,000 goods in transit in the vehicle; $15,000 at a subcontractor; $20,000 in seasonal storage; $5,000 mysterious disappearance, event and aggregate. Excess $2,500 each and every event.
Section 4 covers goods in the insured's custody at either situation, in transit, or with a specialist subcontractor for up to 30 days, whether or not the insured is legally liable.
Not covered under Section 4: inherent vice, unstable dye, delaminating interlining; colour change, loss, bleed or migration through no fault of the insured; wear, tear, shrinkage, loss of finish; damage from correctly following a legible care label; mysterious disappearance above $5,000; goods uncollected more than 3 months after being ready, where notice was given; buttons, beads, trims and zips unless the garment is also damaged; valuables left in pockets; moth and vermin; consequential loss.
Section 2 business interruption: $420,000 gross profit, 12 month indemnity period, 72 hour time excess. Additional increased cost of working $100,000 — temporary steam boiler hire for up to 60 days, outsourced finishing, overtime. Notify Meridian within 48 hours of committing to boiler hire. Claims preparation $25,000; customers' and suppliers' premises $50,000; loss of attraction $25,000; rewriting records $15,000.
Section 3 liability $20,000,000 any one occurrence, defence costs in addition. Excludes property in the insured's control and employee injury.
