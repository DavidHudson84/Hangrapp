SOP 02 Handling a Complaint, first of three parts — authority limits, the first ninety seconds and the problem report.
MSDC-SOP-02, version 3, effective 1 March 2026. Owner Karen Whitfield.
Counter staff take the report only — no admission of liability, no denial, no offer of money, no estimate of blame. Sharon Delaney, Shop Manager, assesses and may settle up to $250 and authorise a re-clean, re-press or alteration at our cost without reference to Karen. Karen Whitfield decides anything over $250, anything that goes out in writing, and anything involving the insurer or Consumer Affairs Victoria.
Prohibited at the counter: "You signed for it"; "That's not our fault"; "That's just wear and tear"; "The label said dry clean, so there's nothing we could have done"; "You'll have to take that up with the manufacturer"; "Everyone gets that with this fabric".
First ninety seconds: stop, take the garment onto the bench, let them finish, then "I'm sorry — that's not what you should have got back from us", say Sharon will look at it properly, ask to keep it, and bag and tag it to the hold rail.
Form MSDC-F10, the problem report, is raised in the app while the customer is there: their account in their own words kept apart from your observation, the location and size of the damage, the value and where that figure came from, and what they want.
The garment is not re-cleaned, spotted, pressed or steamed until it has been assessed and photographed; the only exception is a stabilising step directed by Sharon after ringing Karen.
Complaint photographs the same day, with the 150 mm scale card and a comparison shot of an unaffected part of the garment.
Sharon pulls the docket, the intake record and photographs, the care label as recorded, the route sheet, the machine log and any MSDC-F02.
