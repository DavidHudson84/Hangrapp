SOP 02 Handling a Complaint, third of three parts — deny, settle and goodwill in practice, insurance, escalation and records.
MSDC-SOP-02, version 3, effective 1 March 2026. Owner Karen Whitfield.
Deny is for wear, age, sun, relaxation shrinkage or an inherent characteristic, with nobody to send the customer to. Karen signs it, it cites the DLI TABS bulletin by title, and it carries a practical offer — a hem let down by Anh, a re-press, a repair or a free clean.
Settle: Sharon to $250, Karen above $250 and for anything in writing. The letter owns it in the first paragraph, shows the Guide working, waives the cleaning charge and offers the garment back. Payment by electronic transfer or account credit within five business days of acceptance, coded to claims.
Goodwill is nothing damaged, nobody at fault: Sharon within her $250, no admission of fault, no figure, not entered in the claims register.
Insurance: Meridian Underwriting Australia Pty Ltd (AFSL 241 663), policy MDC-BP-4471822 through Hargreaves & Locke, renewing 30 September 2026. Bailee's liability $50,000 any one event, $25,000 inner limit any one garment, standard excess $2,500. Almost every garment claim is well under the excess and is paid by the business. Below the excess we settle honestly; at or above it nothing is admitted, offered or written until Karen has rung the broker.
To Karen: anything over $250, anything in writing, a rejected assessment, any figure at or above $2,500 (then the broker), and the same day a solicitor's letter, a multi-customer incident or anything from Consumer Affairs Victoria.
Correspondence is acknowledged within five business days and answered within ten. Records kept seven years, route sheets two.
Karen and Sharon review every problem report on the first Tuesday of the month.
