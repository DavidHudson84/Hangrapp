## 2. Nature of this agreement

### 2.1 A licence, not a lease

This agreement grants the Licensee a personal, non-exclusive, contractual right to occupy and use the Licensed Area for the permitted use for the Term. It does not grant, and is not intended to grant, a lease, a tenancy, an easement, or any estate or interest in the Centre or in any land.

### 2.2 What that means

The parties acknowledge and agree that:

(a) the Licensor retains legal possession and control of the whole of the Centre, including the Licensed Area;

(b) the Licensee does not have exclusive possession of the Licensed Area, and the Licensor and its employees, contractors and agents may enter the Licensed Area at any time under clause 8.5;

(c) the Licensor may relocate the Licensee within the Centre under clause 8.6, which is inconsistent with the grant of an estate in a defined parcel of land;

(d) the rights granted are personal to the Licensee, are not assignable except under clause 11, and do not run with the land;

(e) the Licensee must not lodge a caveat or any other dealing against the title to the Centre, and irrevocably authorises the Licensor to withdraw any caveat lodged in breach of this paragraph; and

(f) on the Expiry date, or on earlier termination, the Licensee's right to occupy simply ends, and the Licensee is not entitled to any notice to quit, to compensation for goodwill, or to any right of renewal.

### 2.3 Why the parties have used a licence

The parties have chosen a licence because the Licensed Area is a small serviced position on the mall alignment, formed of counter and racking only, without demising walls, without a separate services connection beyond lighting, power and the hand basin, and because the position may need to be moved to accommodate the Licensor's leasing, refurbishment and remerchandising of the Centre. The Licensee acknowledges that it has been given the opportunity to obtain its own legal and financial advice about the difference between a licence and a retail lease before signing.

### 2.4 Revocation

The Licensor may revoke this licence only in accordance with clause 12. Nothing in clause 2.1 or 2.2 entitles the Licensor to revoke the licence at will during the Term while the Licensee is not in default.

## 3. Grant and term

### 3.1 Grant

In consideration of the Fee and of the Licensee's obligations, the Licensor grants the Licensee the right to occupy and use the Licensed Area for the permitted use in Item 16 during the Term, together with the right to use the common areas of the Centre in common with the Licensor, the other occupants of the Centre and the public.

### 3.2 Term

The Term commences on the date in Item 7 and ends on the date in Item 8, unless ended earlier under this agreement.

### 3.3 Fitout period

The Licensee may occupy the Licensed Area during the fitout period in Item 23 for the purpose of carrying out the approved fitout. No licence fee, turnover fee or promotion levy is payable for the fitout period, but every other obligation of the Licensee under this agreement, including insurance and the Centre rules, applies during it.

### 3.4 No option

There is no option to renew. The Licensee acknowledges that it has no right to a further licence and that the Licensor may licence or lease the Licensed Area to any other person after the Expiry date.

### 3.5 Negotiation for a further licence

If the Licensee wishes to negotiate a further licence it must give the Licensor written notice not later than 31 December 2026. The Licensor must respond in writing within 60 days after receiving that notice, either by offering terms for a further licence or by declining to offer a further licence. Neither party is obliged to agree, and a failure to agree does not give rise to any claim by the Licensee.

### 3.6 Holding over

If the Licensee remains in occupation after the Expiry date with the Licensor's written consent, it does so as a monthly licensee on the terms of this agreement so far as they can apply, at the licence fee, turnover fee and promotion levy payable immediately before the Expiry date, terminable by either party on one month's written notice expiring on any day. Occupation after the Expiry date without consent is a trespass and clause 13.4 applies.

## 4. Fee

### 4.1 Licence fee

The Licensee must pay the licence fee in Item 11 by equal monthly instalments of $1,800.00 plus GST in advance on the first day of each month, without deduction, set-off or demand, by direct debit from the account nominated by the Licensee, or by such other method as the Licensor requires in writing.

### 4.2 Promotion levy

The Licensee must pay the promotion levy in Item 14 with each instalment of the licence fee. The Licensor must apply the promotion levy, together with amounts collected from other occupants, to the marketing, advertising and promotion of the Centre, and must give the Licensee a written summary of promotional expenditure for each accounting year within three months after the end of that year on written request.

### 4.3 Review of the licence fee

The licence fee is fixed for the Term. The promotion levy may be increased by the Licensor once in each accounting year, by not more than 5% or the annual increase in the Consumer Price Index (All Groups, Melbourne), whichever is the greater, on not less than one month's written notice.

### 4.4 No separate outgoings

The licence fee includes the Licensee's contribution to the Centre's outgoings, including rates, taxes, insurance, air conditioning, common area cleaning, security, waste collection and centre management. The Licensee must pay for its own electricity where separately metered, its own telephone and internet, and its own point of sale and merchant fees.

### 4.5 Interest on late payment

If any Fee remains unpaid for 7 days after its due date, the Licensee must pay interest on the unpaid amount from the due date until payment at 2% per annum above the rate fixed from time to time under section 2 of the Penalty Interest Rates Act 1983 (Vic).

### 4.6 Fee is payable regardless of trading

The Fee is payable whether or not the Licensee trades, except where the Licensed Area is unusable for a continuous period of more than 5 days because of an act or omission of the Licensor, in which case the licence fee and promotion levy abate proportionally for the period of unusability.

## 5. Turnover fee, reporting and audit

### 5.1 Gross turnover

Gross turnover means the total of all money and the value of all other consideration received or receivable by the Licensee, or by any person on the Licensee's behalf, from business conducted at or from the Licensed Area, whether for cash or on credit and whether or not the goods or services are delivered or performed at the Licensed Area. Without limiting that meaning, gross turnover includes:

(a) all amounts received at the Licensed Area for dry cleaning, laundering, pressing, alterations and repairs, whether the work is done at the Licensed Area or at the Licensee's plant at 118 Hargreaves Street, Bendigo;

(b) all amounts received at the Licensed Area for garments received at any other place, where the customer pays at the Licensed Area;

(c) the retail sale of garment care products, hangers, covers and similar goods;

(d) deposits forfeited by customers and amounts received for uncollected garments; and

(e) amounts received from insurers, employers or other third parties for work booked in at the Licensed Area.

### 5.2 Exclusions from gross turnover

Gross turnover does not include:

(a) GST;

(b) refunds and allowances actually made to customers for goods or services previously included in gross turnover;

(c) amounts received for garments received at the Licensed Area but paid for at the Licensee's Bendigo premises, provided those amounts are not otherwise included;

(d) proceeds of the sale of the Licensee's own plant, fittings or equipment; and

(e) discounts allowed to staff, to a maximum of 2% of gross turnover in any accounting year.

### 5.3 Turnover fee

Within 30 days after the Licensor gives the Licensee an account calculated from the annual certified statement for an accounting year, the Licensee must pay the Licensor the turnover fee in Item 12, being 4% of the amount by which gross turnover for that accounting year exceeds the turnover threshold of $180,000.00, plus GST. For a part accounting year the turnover threshold is apportioned on a daily basis.

### 5.4 Monthly reporting

The Licensee must give the Licensor, by the 7th day of each month, a written statement of gross turnover at the Licensed Area for the preceding month, signed by the Licensee or by a person authorised by the Licensee. The statement must be given in the form in Annexure C or in such other form as the Licensor reasonably requires, and must be supported by point of sale reports on request.

### 5.5 Annual certified statement

Within 60 days after the end of each accounting year the Licensee must give the Licensor a statement of gross turnover for that accounting year, certified as correct by the Licensee's accountant or by the director of the Licensee, reconciled to the Licensee's business activity statements for the periods falling within that accounting year.

### 5.6 Records

The Licensee must keep at the Licensed Area or at its Bendigo premises, for at least five years, full and accurate records of gross turnover, including point of sale records, daily takings summaries, docket books, banking records and the records required by the taxation laws. The Licensee must operate a point of sale system which records every transaction at the Licensed Area separately from transactions at any other premises of the Licensee.

### 5.7 Late reporting

If the Licensee does not give a monthly statement within 7 days after it is due, the Licensee must pay the Licensor an administration charge of $50.00 plus GST for each week or part week the statement remains outstanding, to a maximum of $500.00 plus GST for any one statement. If a monthly statement is more than 30 days late, the Licensor may estimate gross turnover for that month at 110% of the highest monthly gross turnover reported in the preceding 12 months, and that estimate is taken to be the gross turnover for that month unless the Licensee gives the statement and supporting records within a further 14 days.

### 5.8 Audit

The Licensor may, once in each accounting year and once within 12 months after the end of the Term, have the Licensee's records of gross turnover examined by an accountant nominated by the Licensor. The Licensee must give that accountant access to the records within 10 business days of a written request. The Licensor bears the cost of the examination unless it shows that gross turnover for the period examined was understated by more than 5%, in which case the Licensee must pay the reasonable cost of the examination and any turnover fee shortfall, with interest under clause 4.5, within 14 days.

### 5.9 Confidentiality

The Licensor must keep the Licensee's turnover information confidential and must not disclose it except to its officers, professional advisers, financiers, auditors and the owner of the Centre, or where required by law, or in aggregated form which does not identify the Licensee.

## 6. Trading

### 6.1 Obligation to trade

The Licensee must open the Licensed Area for business and trade continuously during the Licensee's approved trading hours in Item 19 throughout the Term, properly staffed, stocked and lit, with the counter attended at all times while the Licensed Area is open.

### 6.2 Centre trading hours and variation

The centre trading hours are in Item 18. The Licensor has approved in writing, on 14 June 2024, the shorter trading hours in Item 19 for the Licensee, having regard to the service nature of the permitted use. The Licensor may vary the centre trading hours on not less than one month's written notice. The Licensee's approved hours continue to apply unless the Licensor withdraws that approval on not less than three months' written notice, and the Licensee must then trade during the centre trading hours.

### 6.3 Extended trading

The Licensee must open during any additional trading period nominated by the Licensor for the Christmas trading period, being not more than 10 days in any accounting year, on not less than 21 days' written notice.

### 6.4 Failure to trade

If the Licensee fails to open during the approved trading hours, otherwise than because of an event beyond its control, the Licensee must pay the Licensor liquidated damages of $150.00 plus GST for each day or part day on which it fails to open, which the parties agree is a genuine pre-estimate of the loss to the Centre from a dark shop on the mall alignment.

### 6.5 Presentation

The Licensee must keep the Licensed Area clean, tidy and well presented, must keep garment racking behind the counter line and within the Licensed Area, must not place any rack, sign, bin, A-frame or display outside the licence line marked on the plan in Annexure A, and must ensure that its staff wear the uniform and name badge approved by the Licensor.

## 7. Use of the Licensed Area

### 7.1 Permitted use

The Licensee must use the Licensed Area only for the permitted use in Item 16 and for no other purpose.

### 7.2 No plant, no cleaning on site

The Licensee must not, at the Licensed Area:

(a) install or operate any dry cleaning machine, washing machine, tumble dryer, boiler, still, steam generator, compressor or press;

(b) carry out any dry cleaning, wet cleaning, laundering, dyeing or bulk pressing;

(c) bring, store, use or handle any solvent, including perchloroethylene and hydrocarbon solvent, or any solvent-contaminated waste, filter, residue or rag;

(d) use water for any purpose other than hand washing at the hand basin in Item 5, or connect any appliance to the water supply or to the sewer; or

(e) carry out any process which produces steam, odour, heat, noise, effluent or trade waste.

The only steam appliance permitted at the Licensed Area is a single hand-held garment steamer of not more than 2,000 W, filled from the hand basin, used for touch-up finishing only, and switched off and stored behind the counter when not in use.

### 7.3 Garments to be cleaned off site

All garments received at the Licensed Area for cleaning must be transported to the Licensee's plant at Shop 4, 118 Hargreaves Street, Bendigo, and cleaned there or at another licensed plant, and returned to the Licensed Area for collection by the customer. Garments must be transported in a covered vehicle and must not be left in the Centre's common areas, service corridors or loading dock at any time.

### 7.4 Garment storage

The Licensee must keep all garments within the Licensed Area, on the racking, bagged and tagged. The Licensee must not store garments in the Centre's common areas, must not exceed the racking capacity of the Licensed Area, and must remove uncollected garments to its Bendigo premises where they are held for more than 60 days.

### 7.5 Odour

The Licensee must ensure that garments brought into the Centre do not give off solvent odour. Garments must be fully aired at the Licensee's plant before delivery to the Licensed Area. If the Licensor gives the Licensee written notice of a substantiated odour complaint, the Licensee must investigate and report to the Licensor within 5 business days and must take the steps reasonably necessary to prevent a recurrence.

### 7.6 Compliance with laws

The Licensee must comply, at its own cost, with every law, regulation, permit, licence and notice applying to its use of the Licensed Area, including the Occupational Health and Safety Act 2004 (Vic) and the Australian Consumer Law, must display the prices and turnaround times for its services, and must give the Licensor a copy of any notice or order received from a regulator in relation to the Licensed Area within 3 business days.

### 7.7 No nuisance

The Licensee must not cause or allow any nuisance, obstruction, noise, odour or interference with any other occupant of the Centre or with the Centre's customers, must not play amplified music, and must not use any loudspeaker, bell or public address device.

## 8. Fitout, signage, rules and the Licensor's rights

### 8.1 Fitout

The Licensee must carry out the fitout of the Licensed Area in accordance with the Licensor's shopfitting guide and the drawings approved in writing by the Licensor before the Commencing date. The Licensee must not commence any fitout or shopfitting work until the Licensor has approved the drawings, the contractor has given the Licensor a certificate of currency for public liability insurance of not less than $20,000,000 and workers compensation insurance, and the contractor has completed the Centre's contractor induction.

### 8.2 Signage

The Licensee may display one fascia sign to the dimensions, materials, illumination and position approved by the Licensor in writing, in accordance with the Centre's signage code. The Licensee must not display any other sign, poster, decal, price ticket or promotional material visible from the mall without the Licensor's prior written approval, other than a single A4 price list mounted on the counter.

### 8.3 Centre rules

The Licensee must comply, and must ensure its employees and contractors comply, with the Centre rules in Annexure B. The Licensor may amend the Centre rules from time to time on not less than 14 days' written notice, provided the amended rules are not inconsistent with this agreement and apply generally to occupants of the Centre.

### 8.4 Licensor's works

The Licensor may at any time carry out repairs, alterations, refurbishment, extension or redevelopment of the Centre, may close or alter any part of the common areas, may alter the location of entrances, travelators, amenities and car parking, and may erect hoardings and scaffolding, without any abatement of the Fee and without any claim by the Licensee, provided the Licensor uses reasonable endeavours to minimise interference with the Licensee's business and to maintain reasonable access to the Licensed Area.

### 8.5 Access by the Licensor

The Licensor and its employees, contractors and agents may enter the Licensed Area at any time to inspect, to clean, to carry out works, to read meters, to exercise the Licensor's rights under this agreement, to comply with a requirement of a public authority, and in an emergency. The Licensor must, other than in an emergency, use reasonable endeavours to avoid interfering with the Licensee's business.

### 8.6 Relocation

(a) The Licensor may relocate the Licensee to another area within the Centre on not less than three months' written notice.

(b) The new area must be of a comparable size and of a standard of position and customer flow reasonably comparable to the Licensed Area, and must be suitable for the permitted use.

(c) The Licensor must pay the Licensee's reasonable costs of dismantling, moving and reinstalling the counter, racking and signage, and of altering stationery and advertising material, on production of invoices, to a maximum of the amount in Item 27.

(d) On relocation, the licence fee, the promotion levy and the turnover threshold remain unchanged unless the parties agree otherwise in writing, and every other provision of this agreement continues to apply to the new area, which then becomes the Licensed Area.

(e) If the Licensee does not accept the relocation, it may terminate this agreement by written notice given within 21 days after the Licensor's notice, and the termination takes effect on the date nominated in the Licensor's notice for relocation.

### 8.7 No exclusivity

The Licensor gives no exclusivity of trade. The Licensor may licence or lease any other part of the Centre to a person carrying on a business which competes with the Licensee's business, and the Licensee has no claim in respect of that.

## 9. Services, cleaning and waste

### 9.1 Services

The Licensor must provide lighting, power, air conditioning to the mall, and water to the hand basin, during the centre trading hours. The Licensor is not liable for any interruption to a service which is beyond its reasonable control, and no interruption entitles the Licensee to terminate or to any abatement except under clause 4.6.

The Licensor cleans the common areas. The Licensee must clean the Licensed Area daily, including the counter, racking, floor covering and glass within the Licensed Area, and must keep the hand basin and its trap clean and clear.

The Licensee must remove all its waste from the Licensed Area to the Centre's waste compactor at the loading dock, at the times allowed by the Centre rules, and must separate recyclable material. The Licensee must not place any garment bag, hanger box, packaging or other waste in the mall bins, and must not bring into the Centre any waste generated at its Bendigo premises.

### 9.4 Deliveries

Deliveries to and collections from the Licensed Area must be made through the service corridor at the times allowed by the Centre rules, and not through the mall during centre trading hours. Garment trolleys must be covered and must not be left unattended in the service corridor.

## 10. Insurance and indemnity

The Licensee must take out and keep current throughout the Term, with an insurer authorised to carry on insurance business in Australia:

(a) public and products liability insurance for not less than the amount in Item 21 for any one occurrence, noting the interest of the Licensor and of the owner of the Centre;

(b) insurance of customers' goods in the Licensee's care, custody or control at the Licensed Area for not less than the amount in Item 22;

### 10.4 Conduct affecting insurance

The Licensee must not do anything at the Licensed Area which invalidates or increases the premium of the Licensor's insurance, and must pay any increase in that premium caused by its use of the Licensed Area.

### 10.5 Indemnity

The Licensee indemnifies the Licensor and the owner of the Centre against all liability, loss, cost and expense in connection with loss of or damage to property, or injury to or death of any person, arising from the Licensee's use or occupation of the Licensed Area, from any act or omission of the Licensee or of its employees, contractors or customers, or from any breach of this agreement by the Licensee, except to the extent caused by the negligence or default of the Licensor.

### 10.6 Release

The Licensee occupies the Licensed Area at its own risk and releases the Licensor from liability for loss of or damage to the Licensee's property, the Licensee's customers' goods, or the Licensee's business, however caused, except to the extent caused by the negligence or default of the Licensor.

### 10.7 Customers' goods

The Licensee is solely responsible to its customers for garments received at the Licensed Area. The Licensor is not a bailee of any garment and has no liability to any customer of the Licensee.

## 11. Assignment

### 11.1 Personal right

This licence is personal to the Licensee. The Licensee must not assign, sublicense, part with possession of, share the occupation of, or otherwise deal with its rights under this agreement, and must not permit any other person to trade from the Licensed Area.

### 11.2 Change in control

### 11.3 Sale of the Licensee's business

The Licensor must not unreasonably refuse, but may require that the purchaser demonstrate financial standing and retailing experience at least equivalent to the Licensee's, that the purchaser provide equivalent security and a guarantee from its principals, that the Licensee pay the Licensor's reasonable costs, and that the Licensee and the Guarantor remain liable for all amounts accrued to the date the new licence is granted.

### 11.4 Licensor may deal freely

The Licensor may sell, transfer, mortgage or otherwise deal with the Centre or with its interest in it. The Licensee must, on request, sign any document reasonably required to acknowledge the interest of a purchaser, mortgagee or new manager, and this agreement continues on the same terms.

## 12. Default and termination

### 12.1 Default

The Licensee is in default if:

(a) any Fee remains unpaid for 7 days after its due date, whether or not demanded;

(b) the Licensee breaches any other provision of this agreement and does not remedy the breach within 14 days after written notice;

(c) the Licensee is wound up, has a controller or administrator appointed, or is unable to pay its debts as they fall due;

(d) the Licensee ceases to trade from the Licensed Area for 7 consecutive days without the Licensor's written consent;

(e) the Licensee gives the Licensor a turnover statement which is materially false; or

(f) the Guarantor dies or becomes bankrupt and a replacement guarantor acceptable to the Licensor is not provided within 30 days.

### 12.2 Essential terms

Clauses 4.1 (licence fee), 5.4 and 5.5 (turnover reporting), 6.1 (obligation to trade), 7.1 and 7.2 (use, and no plant or cleaning on site), 10.1 (insurance) and 11.1 (assignment) are essential terms, and breach of an essential term is a repudiation of this agreement.

### 12.3 Immediate termination

The Licensor may terminate this agreement immediately by written notice, without any notice to remedy, if the Licensee breaches clause 7.2, if the Licensee's occupation creates an immediate risk to the health or safety of any person in the Centre, or if the Licensee becomes insolvent.

### 12.4 Termination for other default

For any other default the Licensor may terminate this agreement by written notice after the time for remedy in clause 12.1 has passed. On termination the Licensee's right to occupy ends immediately and the Licensee must vacate the Licensed Area in accordance with clause 13.

### 12.5 Damages

Termination does not affect the Licensor's right to recover the Fee accrued to the date of termination, interest under clause 4.5, and damages for the loss of the benefit of this agreement for what would have been the remainder of the Term. The Licensor must take reasonable steps to mitigate its loss.

### 12.6 Licensor may remedy

If the Licensee fails to do anything this agreement requires it to do, the Licensor may, after giving 7 days' written notice or immediately in an emergency, do it, and the reasonable cost is payable by the Licensee on demand.

### 12.7 Termination by the Licensee

The Licensee may terminate this agreement under clause 8.6(e), and may otherwise terminate on 6 months' written notice given at any time after 30 June 2026, in which case the Licensee must pay the Licensor an amount equal to two months' licence fee and promotion levy on the date the termination takes effect.

### 12.8 No waiver

Acceptance of the Fee after a default, or delay in acting on a default, does not waive the default or affect the Licensor's rights.

## 13. End of term and make good

### 13.1 Vacating

On the Expiry date or on earlier termination the Licensee must remove all its garments, stock, records, equipment, counter, back counter, racking, signage and fittings, and give the Licensed Area back to the Licensor clean and free of rubbish.

## ANNEXURE B — CENTRE RULES

1. Trading. Trade only during your approved hours. The counter must be attended whenever the shop is open.

2. Presentation. Keep the counter, racking and fascia clean and in good order. No handwritten signs. No stock, racks or bins forward of the licence line.

3. Staff. Staff must wear the approved uniform and a name badge, must not eat, drink or use a mobile telephone at the counter, and must park in the staff parking area at the southern end of the car park.

4. Deliveries. All deliveries and collections through the service corridor and loading dock only, before 9:00 am or after 5:30 pm, other than by written approval of the Centre Manager. No garment trolleys through the mall during centre trading hours. Trolleys must be covered.

5. Waste. All waste to the compactor at the loading dock between 8:00 am and 9:00 am or between 5:30 pm and 6:00 pm. Cardboard flattened. No waste from any other premises may be brought into the Centre.

6. Noise and odour. No amplified music, bells or public address devices. Garments must not give off solvent odour.

7. Services. No connection to any water, drainage or gas service. No appliance may be connected to the hand basin. No portable heater, hot plate, kettle, iron or steam appliance other than the approved hand steamer.

8. Safety. Keep the fire exits, the service corridor and the sprinkler and detector heads clear. Report every incident, injury and spill to the Centre Management Office on the day it occurs. Complete the Centre's contractor induction before any contractor works on site.

9. Security. Lock the Licensed Area and secure the racking at close of trade. Keys and after-hours contact details for at least two people must be lodged with the Centre Management Office and kept current.

10. Promotions. Take part in the Centre's promotional activities as reasonably requested. Do not distribute leaflets in the mall or the car park without the Centre Manager's written approval.

11. Trading disputes. Direct any complaint about another occupant to the Centre Management Office and not to that occupant.

12. Reporting. Lodge the monthly turnover statement by the 7th of each month through the Centre's occupant portal or by email to the Centre Manager.
