### 1.2 Recitals

This Agreement commenced on 1 February 2025 and continues for two years,
expiring on 31 January 2027. After that date it continues month to month on the
same terms unless terminated under clause 13 or replaced by a further written
agreement.

### 1.4 Scope

This Agreement covers supply to both the Plant and the Drop Store. It does not
cover solvent, process chemistry or waste removal, which the Customer sources
under a separate agreement with another supplier.

**Branded Stock** means polythene, gown bags and laundry bags printed with the
Customer's artwork under clause 10.

clause 4, the current version being List 3 effective 1 February 2026.

Standing Order.

## 3. Supply and ordering

### 3.1 Standing weekly order

The Customer maintains a Standing Order, set out in Annexure A, which the
Supplier picks, invoices and delivers each week without further instruction. The
Standing Order may be varied by the Customer in writing at any time, and a
variation takes effect from the first delivery falling more than two Business
Days after the Supplier receives it.

The Supplier will review the Standing Order with the Customer once each year
before the price review under clause 8, and will recommend changes where the
usage pattern shown by the account no longer matches the Standing Order.

### 3.2 Top-up orders

Top-Up Orders may be placed by telephone, by email to
sales@trenthamsupply.com.au or through the Supplier's trade portal by 15:00 on
the Business Day before the scheduled delivery. Lines held in the Bendigo East
warehouse are delivered on the next scheduled delivery run.

Indent lines, Branded Stock and press covers cut to a machine pattern are quoted
with a lead time when the order is acknowledged, and are ordered subject to
clause 9.2 and clause 10.

### 3.3 Order authority

The persons authorised to order on the account are Karen Whitfield, Nadia
Farrell and Sharon Delaney. The Supplier may accept an order from any person at
either site who identifies as one of the authorised persons and is not obliged
to verify their authority. The Customer must notify the Supplier in writing of
any change to the authorised persons.

### 3.4 Availability and substitution

Where a line is unavailable the Supplier will notify the Customer before
delivery and may substitute an equivalent line at the same or a lower price. A
substitution made without notice may be returned for full credit under
clause 9.1. Where a line is discontinued the Supplier will give the Customer not
less than sixty days' notice and will offer a replacement line and a sample.

### 3.5 Samples

The Supplier will supply a sample of any new or substituted line at no charge on
request. A sample supplied for evaluation remains the property of the Supplier
until the Customer places an order for that line.

## 5. Delivery and freight

### 5.1 Schedule

The Plant is delivered every Wednesday between 8:00 and 11:00 to the rear
laneway off Bath Lane. The Drop Store is delivered fortnightly on Tuesdays
between 9:00 and 11:00 to the centre loading dock at Lansell Square and
trolleyed to the tenancy. Where a scheduled delivery day falls on a public
holiday the delivery is made on the next Business Day.

### 5.2 Freight

Freight is free on orders of $350.00 or more excluding GST. Orders below that
value carry a delivery charge of $28.00. Same-day or after-hours delivery is
$85.00 where the Supplier can meet it. A delivery to the Drop Store outside the
fortnightly run carries the $28.00 charge regardless of order value.

### 5.3 Receipt and shortages

The Customer must check each delivery against the delivery docket at the time of
delivery. Shortages, incorrect lines and visible damage must be noted on the
docket and reported to the Supplier within two Business Days, after which the
delivery is taken to have been accepted as delivered.

### 5.4 Access

The Supplier's driver must not block the Bath Lane roller door for longer than
the delivery requires, and must not leave stock in the laneway unattended. Stock
is placed in the rear store, clear of the solvent store and clear of the
compressor bay.

## 6. Minimum order

The minimum value for any order, including a Top-Up Order, is $150.00 excluding
GST. The Standing Order satisfies the minimum for the week in which it is
delivered. Orders below the minimum are held and consolidated with the next
delivery unless the Customer asks for immediate delivery and pays the freight
charge under clause 5.2.

## 7. Rebates

### 7.1 Rebate tiers

A rebate is payable on the Customer's total net purchases in each Rebate Year,
calculated on the tiers below and applied to the whole of the annual spend, not
to the excess over each threshold.

| Annual net spend, excluding GST | Rebate |
|---|---|
| Below $12,000 | Nil |
| $12,000 to $17,999 | 1.5% |
| $18,000 to $23,999 | 2.5% |
| $24,000 to $29,999 | 3.5% |
| $30,000 to $39,999 | 5.0% |
| $40,000 and above | 6.0% |

### 7.2 Calculation and payment

The rebate is issued as a credit note against the Customer's account in August
following the end of the Rebate Year. Freight charges, printing set-up charges,
tagging guns and other equipment, and Consignment Stock not yet drawn down are
excluded from the calculation. Credits issued for returns are deducted from net
spend.

No rebate is payable while any amount on the account is more than sixty days
overdue, and a rebate otherwise payable is applied first against any overdue
amount.

## 8. Price review

Prices are reviewed once each year with effect from 1 February. The Supplier
must give the Customer written notice of the reviewed prices not less than
thirty days before they take effect. No line item may increase at a review by
more than the increase in CPI for the twelve months to the September quarter
immediately preceding the review, plus two percentage points.

Between reviews the Supplier may pass on a documented increase in the cost of
polythene resin or of imported hanger wire on thirty days' written notice,
limited to the lines affected and supported by the mill or supplier notice
relied on. The Customer may reject an increase notified under this paragraph by
giving notice within fourteen days, in which case the affected lines continue to
be supplied at the old price until the Supplier's current stock holding is
exhausted and are then withdrawn from the Price List.

The wire hanger lines were last increased at the review effective 1 February
2026, from $40.50 to $42.00 for the 16" line. The polythene lines were held at
the 1 February 2025 prices at that review.

## 9. Returns and credits

### 9.1 General returns

Unopened stock in saleable condition may be returned within thirty days of
delivery for full credit where the return is the result of a Supplier error, and
within ninety days for credit less a restocking charge of 15% where the Customer
has over-ordered. Returns must be booked with the Supplier in advance and are
collected on the next scheduled delivery run. Stock returned dirty, water
marked, crushed or without its original packaging is not credited.

### 9.2 Printed and made-to-order items

Branded Stock, printed laundry bags and press covers cut to a machine's pattern
are made to order and are **not returnable**, except for a manufacturing fault
or a printing error against the artwork approved under clause 10.2. A claim for
a printing error must be made within fourteen days of delivery and must identify
the roll or carton number printed on the packaging.

### 9.3 Faulty goods

Faulty goods are credited or replaced at the Customer's election. The Supplier
may require the return of a sample and, for wire hanger faults, the batch label
from the affected box. A claim for a latent fault in press pads or covers must
be made within ninety days of delivery.

## 10. Branded stock

### 10.1 Printed lines

The Supplier prints "Main Street Dry Cleaners — Bendigo's since 1987" together
with the Customer's telephone number and web address on the 21" and 40"
polythene and on the 60" gown bag. Printing is one colour, single side, repeated
at 900 mm intervals along the roll. The artwork specification is at Annexure D.

### 10.2 Minimum print run, artwork and lead time

The minimum print run for any branded line is 5,000 metres of polythene or 5,000
gown bags. The lead time is six weeks from written artwork approval. The
Customer must approve a printed proof in writing before the run is scheduled,
and the Supplier is not liable for an error that was present in the approved
proof.

Where a run is scheduled and the Customer cancels after the plate has been
mounted, the Customer must pay the mounting charge of $180.00.

### 10.3 Print charges and plate ownership

A one-off plate charge of $680.00 applies to each new artwork. Printed polythene
and printed gown bags are supplied at the premiums set out in clauses 4.3 and
4.4.

Title to the print plate passes to the Customer twelve months after the plate
charge is paid, after which the Supplier holds the plate as bailee for the
Customer and must release it to the Customer or to a nominated printer on
request. The plate charge for the Customer's current artwork was paid on
14 March 2025 and title passed on 14 March 2026.

### 10.4 Change of artwork

A change of artwork requires a new plate and a new plate charge. The Supplier
will run out existing printed stock before introducing new artwork unless the
Customer directs otherwise in writing and accepts the cost of the stock on hand.

## 11. Consignment stock

### 11.1 Lines held

The Supplier holds the following lines at the Plant on consignment: wire hangers
in the 14", 16" and 18" lengths, heavy shirt hangers in the 16" length, and the
21" and 40" polythene. Title to Consignment Stock remains with the Supplier
until it is counted as drawn down.

Risk in Consignment Stock passes to the Customer on delivery. The Customer must
store it under cover, clear of the floor, away from the solvent store and away
from any source of heat, and must not move it to another site.

### 11.2 Counting

Consignment Stock is counted on the first Wednesday of each month by the
Supplier's driver together with a member of the Customer's staff, and the count
sheet is signed by both. Stock drawn down since the previous count is invoiced
on the count date on the ordinary payment terms.

A discrepancy of more than 2% between the count and the expected draw-down is
investigated jointly before the invoice issues. A discrepancy that cannot be
reconciled is invoiced to the Customer.

### 11.3 Limits and removal

The consigned holding must not exceed six weeks of average usage measured over
the preceding three months. The Supplier may reduce the holding on notice, and
may remove Consignment Stock on seven days' notice if the account is suspended
under clause 12 or if this Agreement is terminated.

## 12. Payment

### 12.1 Terms

Payment is due thirty days from the end of the month in which the invoice is
dated, by electronic funds transfer to the account nominated on the invoice.
Prices exclude GST, which is payable in addition and is shown separately on each
tax invoice.

### 12.2 Interest and suspension

Interest accrues on any amount not paid by its due date at 1.0% per month,
calculated daily from the due date until payment. If any amount is more than
forty-five days overdue the Supplier may suspend supply, including the Standing
Order, on written notice until the account is brought current.

### 12.3 Credit limit

The account operates to a credit limit of $6,000. The Supplier may decline to
supply on credit above that limit and may require payment in advance for an
order that would take the account past it.

## 13. Warranty, liability and termination

### 13.1 Warranty

The Supplier warrants that the goods will correspond with their description,
will be of acceptable quality, and will be free from defects in materials and
workmanship. Nothing in this Agreement excludes, restricts or modifies a
guarantee, right or remedy conferred by the Australian Consumer Law that cannot
lawfully be excluded.

### 13.2 Liability

Where the Supplier's liability may lawfully be limited, it is limited at the
Supplier's option to replacing the goods, supplying equivalent goods or paying
the cost of replacement. The Supplier's aggregate liability under or in
connection with this Agreement in any twelve month period is limited to the
total amount paid by the Customer to the Supplier in that period.

Neither party is liable to the other for loss of profit, loss of revenue, loss
of goodwill or any indirect or consequential loss. The Supplier is not liable
for damage to a garment arising from the Customer's choice of cover, bag or
hanger for a particular fabric or finish.

### 13.3 Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other not less than
ninety days' written notice. Either party may terminate immediately by written
notice if the other commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within
fourteen days of written notice requiring it to do so, or becomes insolvent, has
a controller or administrator appointed, or resolves to wind up.

### 13.4 Effect of termination

On termination all amounts owing become immediately due and payable. The
Supplier may collect all Consignment Stock not yet drawn down within fourteen
days of the effective date. The Customer must pay for Branded Stock already
produced, and for Branded Stock in production against an approved artwork, up to
the minimum run quantity. Any print plate to which title has passed to the
Customer is released to the Customer at the Customer's cost.
