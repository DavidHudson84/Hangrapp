### 1.2 Recitals

A. The Supplier formulates, imports and distributes dry cleaning solvents,
process chemistry and ancillary consumables to the textile care industry in
Victoria, and arranges the removal of prescribed industrial waste generated by
its customers through a permissioned transporter.

B. The Customer operates a dry cleaning plant at Shop 4, 118 Hargreaves Street,
Bendigo VIC 3550 (**the Site**), which houses a perchloroethylene dry cleaning
machine, a hydrocarbon dry cleaning machine, a solvent recovery still, wet
cleaning equipment and a bunded solvent store.

C. The Customer wishes to appoint the Supplier as its supplier of solvent and
process chemistry, and to have the Supplier arrange removal of the prescribed
industrial waste generated at the Site, and the Supplier is willing to do so on
the terms set out below.

### 1.3 Term

This Agreement commenced on 1 April 2024 and continues for an initial term of
three years, expiring on 31 March 2027 (**the Initial Term**), unless terminated
earlier under clause 9.

### 1.4 Renewal

At the end of the Initial Term this Agreement continues automatically for
successive rolling periods of twelve months on the same terms, as varied by any
price schedule then in force, unless either party gives written notice of
termination under clause 9.1. No further act of either party is needed to effect
a rolling renewal.

### 1.5 Exclusivity

For so long as this Agreement is on foot the Customer must purchase all of its
requirements for perchloroethylene, hydrocarbon solvent, charge soap, sizing and
spotting chemistry from the Supplier, other than incidental quantities purchased
in an emergency where the Supplier has confirmed in writing that it cannot
supply within the required time.

## 2. Definitions

**ADG Code** means the Australian Code for the Transport of Dangerous Goods by
Road & Rail, Edition 7.9, as amended or replaced.

**Business Day** means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday
observed in Bendigo, Victoria.

**Collection** means an attendance at the Site by the Waste Contractor to remove
Prescribed Industrial Waste under clause 5.

**CPI** means the Consumer Price Index, All Groups, Melbourne, published by the
Australian Bureau of Statistics.

**Dangerous Goods** has the meaning given in the Dangerous Goods Act 1985 (Vic)
and the Dangerous Goods (Storage and Handling) Regulations 2022 (Vic).

**Goods** means the solvents, process chemistry, filtration media, still bags,
personal protective equipment and other products supplied by the Supplier under
this Agreement.

**Order Acknowledgement** means the written confirmation issued by the Supplier
accepting an order, stating the lines, quantities, prices and delivery date.

**PIW** or **Prescribed Industrial Waste** means industrial waste that is
prescribed as such under the Environment Protection Act 2017 (Vic) and the
Environment Protection Regulations 2021 (Vic), including reportable priority
waste.

**Price Schedule** means the schedule of prices in force from time to time under
clause 4, the current version being Schedule B effective 1 April 2026.

**SDS** means a Safety Data Sheet prepared in accordance with the Code of
Practice for the Preparation of Safety Data Sheets for Hazardous Chemicals.

**Site** means Shop 4, 118 Hargreaves Street, Bendigo VIC 3550, including the
rear laneway access off Bath Lane.

**Trained Receiver** means a person nominated by the Customer under clause 3.7
who has been trained to receive, check and place a consignment of Dangerous
Goods and to respond to a spill.

**Waste Contractor** means Sovereign Environmental Services Pty Ltd, or such
other EPA permissioned transporter as the Supplier may appoint under clause 5.2.

**Waste Transport Certificate** means the transport record required for the
transport of reportable priority waste under the Environment Protection
Regulations 2021 (Vic), whether created in the EPA waste tracking system or in
any successor system.

## 3. Supply obligations

### 3.1 Ordering

The Customer may place orders by telephone to the Supplier's Campbellfield order
desk, by email to orders@solvexchemicals.com.au, or through the Supplier's
online account portal. Each order must state the Agreement number
SVX-2024-0318, the product code and description, the quantity, and the name of
the person placing the order. An order is not accepted until the Supplier issues
an Order Acknowledgement.

The Customer must nominate in writing the persons authorised to place orders on
its account. As at the date of Schedule B the authorised persons are Karen
Whitfield, Nadia Farrell and Peter Nguyen. The Supplier may accept an order from
any person who identifies themselves as one of the authorised persons and is not
obliged to verify their authority.

### 3.2 Lead times

The Supplier will deliver stock lines within five Business Days of accepting an
order for delivery to Bendigo and surrounding districts. Non-stock lines,
including special order spotting chemistry, non-standard filtration media and
any product identified in the Price Schedule as indent, will be delivered within
ten Business Days of order acceptance.

Lead times run from acceptance, not from the placing of the order, and do not
run while an account is suspended under clause 7.4.

### 3.3 Minimum order

The Customer must purchase not less than four 200 L drums of perchloroethylene
in each twelve month period commencing 1 April. If in any such period the
Customer purchases fewer than four drums, the Supplier may invoice the Customer
for the shortfall at the drum price then in force, and the Customer must pay
that invoice on the ordinary terms. Drums purchased in excess of four in a
period do not carry forward.

The minimum order value for any single delivery is $250 excluding GST. Orders
below that value will be held and consolidated with the next order unless the
Customer asks for immediate delivery and pays the delivery fee set out in the
Price Schedule.

### 3.4 Delivery windows

Deliveries to the Site are made to the rear laneway off Bath Lane between 7:00
and 12:00 on Tuesdays and Thursdays. The Customer must ensure the laneway is
clear of vehicles and stock, and that a Trained Receiver is present for the
whole of the nominated window. Where the Supplier attends within the window and
cannot deliver, a failed delivery fee applies.

Deliveries outside the standard windows may be arranged by agreement and attract
the out-of-window fee in the Price Schedule. The Supplier does not deliver
solvent to the Site during retail trading hours on a Saturday.

### 3.5 Title and risk

Risk in the Goods passes to the Customer on the Goods being unloaded at the
Site. Title in the Goods does not pass until the Supplier has received payment
in full of all amounts owing by the Customer on any account. Until title passes
the Customer holds the Goods as bailee, must store them so that they are
identifiable as the Supplier's property, and the Supplier may enter the Site
during trading hours on reasonable notice to recover them.

This clause creates a security interest for the purposes of the Personal
Property Securities Act 2009 (Cth) and the Customer consents to the Supplier
registering that interest on the Personal Property Securities Register.

### 3.6 Dangerous goods delivery

Perchloroethylene is consigned as UN 1897, TETRACHLOROETHYLENE, Class 6.1,
Packing Group III. Amyl acetate is consigned as UN 1104, AMYL ACETATES, Class 3,
Packing Group III. Hydrocarbon solvent is a C1 combustible liquid under AS 1940
and is not a dangerous good for transport at the flash point supplied.

The Supplier will:

(a) transport all Dangerous Goods in accordance with the ADG Code, in packaging
that is UN performance tested, correctly marked and within its test life;

(b) ensure each vehicle carries the transport documentation, emergency
information and equipment required by the ADG Code for the load carried;

(c) ensure drivers hold the licences and dangerous goods driver competencies
required for the load; and

(d) provide the Customer with the current SDS for each product on or before
first delivery and again on any revision.

### 3.7 Customer's receiving obligations

The Customer must at all times maintain at the Site:

(a) a bunded receiving and storage area for solvent drums, with a bund capacity
of not less than 110% of the largest container stored plus 25% of the aggregate
capacity of all other containers, impervious to the solvents stored, kept free
of accumulated water, and with no drain to sewer or stormwater;

(b) segregation of Class 3 and Class 6.1 goods from incompatible materials in
accordance with the Dangerous Goods (Storage and Handling) Regulations 2022
(Vic);

(c) a Trained Receiver present at each delivery who is competent to check the
consignment against the delivery documentation, to refuse a damaged or leaking
container, to direct the placement of drums, and to respond to a spill; and

(d) spill containment materials sufficient for a 200 L release, and an eyewash
and safety shower within ten seconds' travel of the decanting point.

The Customer confirms that the aggregate quantity of Dangerous Goods held at the
Site is below the placarding quantity for the classes held, and must notify the
Supplier in writing before that ceases to be the case.

The Supplier's driver may refuse to unload if the receiving area is not
compliant, if no Trained Receiver is present, or if unloading cannot be carried
out safely. A refusal on any of those grounds is treated as a failed delivery.

### 3.8 Decanting and transfer

Solvent must be transferred from drum to machine by closed coupling or by a
grounded and bonded pump. The Customer must not decant solvent by gravity into
an open vessel, must not leave a transfer unattended, and must bond the drum and
the receiving vessel before transferring hydrocarbon solvent. Empty drums must
be closed, bung fitted, and returned to the bunded store pending collection.

## 5. Waste removal

### 5.1 Waste covered

The Supplier will arrange the removal from the Site of:

(a) still residue and separator water sludge from the Union XL-800 and the Bowe
MiniStill, classified as reportable priority waste, waste code N220, halogenated
organic solvents and residues;

(b) spent cartridge filters, spin disc cartridges, lint and button trap waste
contaminated with perchloroethylene, waste code N220;

(c) hydrocarbon still residue and spent filtration media from the Realstar
HM-450, waste code N205, non-halogenated organic solvents and residues; and

(d) spent activated carbon from the vapour adsorber, waste code N220.

Contaminated absorbents, rags and used personal protective equipment may be
included in a Collection under waste code N220 where they are packaged
separately and labelled as solid waste.

### 5.2 Subcontracting

The Supplier subcontracts the transport of PIW under this clause to Sovereign
Environmental Services Pty Ltd, an EPA permissioned transporter, and remains
responsible to the Customer for the performance of the Waste Contractor. The
Supplier may substitute another EPA permissioned transporter on written notice
to the Customer.

### 5.3 Packaging and labelling

The Customer must package waste for collection in the Supplier's supplied 200 L
open-head recovery drums or 20 L pails, in each case closed, sealed and not
overfilled beyond 90% of capacity. Each container must carry a durable label
stating the generator's name and address, the description of the waste, the
waste code, the date the container was first filled and the class label required
by the ADG Code.

The Customer must not mix waste streams, must not place free liquid in a
container nominated for solid waste, and must not place any waste in a container
that is corroded, dented at a seam, or missing a closure ring.

### 5.4 Waste Transport Certificates

For each Collection the Waste Contractor will initiate a Waste Transport
Certificate in the EPA waste tracking system. The Customer, as generator, must
verify and confirm the generator section of the certificate, including the waste
code, the quantity, the containers and the destination premises, before the load
leaves the Site.

The Customer must retain a copy of every certificate and must reconcile the
receiving section of each certificate when it is completed by the receiving
premises. The Supplier will provide the Customer with a consolidated
reconciliation of certificates and receipts within fourteen days of the end of
each quarter.

### 5.5 Collection schedule

Scheduled Collections are made quarterly, in the weeks commencing the second
Monday of March, June, September and December, from the rear laneway off Bath
Lane between 7:00 and 12:00.

Where the volume of PIW held at the Site exceeds four 200 L drums, or where any
waste has been held on site for a period approaching the limit permitted without
a permission, the Customer must request an on-call Collection and the Supplier
will attend within five Business Days.

Without limiting that acknowledgement, the Customer remains solely responsible
for correctly classifying the waste it produces, for accurately describing it,

The Supplier may refuse to accept a container for Collection where the contents
do not match the description given, where the container is unfit, or where

refusal on any of those grounds attracts the rejected container handling fee and

## 6. Safety and compliance

### 6.1 Safety Data Sheets

The Supplier will provide the current SDS for each product on or before first
supply, on each revision, and at any time on request. No SDS supplied under this
Agreement will be more than five years old at the date of supply.

### 6.2 Chemical register

The Customer must maintain a register of all hazardous chemicals held at the
Site, listing the product, the hazard classification, the maximum quantity held,
the location and the SDS revision date, and must keep the register readily
accessible to workers and to emergency services. The register current as at the
date of Schedule B is at Annexure 1.

### 6.3 Training

The Customer must ensure that every worker who handles, decants, receives or
disposes of the Goods has been trained in the hazards of the product, the
control measures, the use of personal protective equipment and the spill and
emergency procedures, and that records of that training are kept. The Supplier
will provide one product handling refresher session at the Site each year at no
charge.

### 6.4 Incident notification

The Customer must notify the Supplier by telephone within two hours, and in
writing within twenty-four hours, of any spill, escape, fire, exposure or
near-miss at the Site involving the Goods or the waste, and of any notice,
direction or infringement issued to the Customer by EPA Victoria or WorkSafe
Victoria that relates to the Goods, the waste or their storage.

### 6.5 Emergency response

The Supplier maintains a 24 hour technical emergency line on 1800 SOLVEX for
advice on spill, exposure and fire involving its products. The Customer must
keep the current SDS and the emergency information holder at the Bath Lane entry
so that they are accessible to emergency services without entering the plant.

### 6.6 Product stewardship

The Supplier will accept the return of unopened Goods that are within date and
in saleable condition, will accept the return of empty containers supplied by
it, and will manage returned product and containers through a licensed
reprocessing or disposal route. The Supplier will notify the Customer of any
change in a product's classification, formulation or regulatory status that
affects the way the Customer must store, use or dispose of it.

### 6.7 Storage audit

The Supplier may audit the Customer's storage, decanting and waste holding
arrangements once in each twelve month period on ten Business Days' notice, and
at any time following an incident notified under clause 6.4.

If an audit finds the arrangements non-compliant, the Supplier will give the
Customer written notice of the findings and a period of not less than
twenty-eight days to rectify them. If the non-compliance is not rectified within
that period, the Supplier may suspend supply of the affected Goods until it is,
and no failure to supply during a suspension under this clause is a breach by
the Supplier. Where the non-compliance presents an immediate risk of harm, the
Supplier may suspend supply immediately and notify the Customer of the reason in
writing within one Business Day.

## 9. Termination

### 9.1 Termination on notice

Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time by giving the other not
less than sixty days' written notice. A notice given under this clause takes
effect at the end of the sixty day period whether or not the Initial Term has
expired.

### 9.2 Termination for breach

Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the
other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within fourteen
days of written notice requiring it to do so, or commits a material breach that
is not capable of remedy, or becomes insolvent, has a controller or
administrator appointed, or resolves to wind up.

### 9.3 Effect of termination

On termination:

(a) all amounts owing to the Supplier become immediately due and payable;

(b) the Customer must pay for all Goods delivered and all Collections performed
before the effective date of termination;

(c) the Customer may retain drums of solvent on hand for which it has paid in
full, and title to those drums passes on payment;

(d) the Supplier will, at the Customer's request and cost, attend the Site
within twenty Business Days of termination to perform a final Collection of all
PIW then held, charged at the on-call rate;

(e) the Customer must return all deposit-bearing containers, whether full or
empty, within thirty days, and the Supplier will credit the deposit for each
container returned in sound, closed and drainable condition. A deposit is
forfeited for any container not returned within that period, or returned
punctured, seam-split, contaminated with a foreign substance or with its closure
ring missing; and

(f) clauses 5.7, 5.8, 6.4, 7.3, 7.6, 8 and 10 survive termination.
