Curtain work is booked, not walk-in, and takes five to seven business days. Take-down and re-hang is by appointment, Tuesday and Thursday, and is charged in 30-minute increments after the first hour.

**Relaxation shrinkage — read this before you book.** Curtain fabric that has hung in a window for years is held stretched by its own weight and by heat. Cleaning releases that tension and the fabric returns towards its true length. Shrinkage of 3% to 5% is normal, and up to 10% happens on loose-weave, cotton-blend and older sun-damaged fabric. On a 2.4 m drop, 5% is 120 mm. We cannot prevent it, we cannot predict it for your particular fabric, and we do not accept a claim for it. We can re-hem or let down a hem at the rates in section 9 where there is fabric to work with. Sun-rotted fabric may also split on handling; we will point this out at booking if we can see it, but rot is often invisible until the fabric is moved.

## 14. OUR GUARANTEE, AT-RISK WORK AND CLAIMS

**The guarantee.** If you are not happy with the cleaning or the finish, bring the garment back within 7 days of collection with the docket and we will re-clean and re-finish it at no charge. If we still cannot get it right we will refund what you paid for that garment. This guarantee is in addition to your rights under the Australian Consumer Law, which cannot be excluded.

**At-risk work, in two sentences.** Some garments carry a real risk of damage in cleaning that no cleaner can remove — unlabelled or wrongly labelled fabric, glued or loose beading and trim, bonded and coated fabric, prints and flocking, sun-degraded fibre, existing dye instability, and curtains subject to relaxation shrinkage — and where we identify that risk we will tell you, price the work on inspection, and ask you to sign an at-risk docket before we start. Signing the at-risk docket means you have asked us to proceed knowing the garment may be damaged by a defect that was already in it, and it does not relieve us of responsibility for damage caused by our own error.

**Claims.** If something has gone wrong, tell us as soon as you notice it and bring the garment and the docket to Hargreaves Street. Sharon Delaney or Karen Whitfield will look at it with you, and we will tell you within five business days whether we accept the claim, whether we want an independent textile assessment, or why we do not accept it. Our full process, the assessment steps, how depreciation is worked out, and what happens if you disagree with our decision are set out in our **Fair Claims Guide**, available at the counter at both sites and at www.mainstreetdrycleaners.com.au. Ask for it — it is two pages and it is written to be read.
