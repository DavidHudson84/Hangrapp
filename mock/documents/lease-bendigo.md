## PART 2 — OPERATIVE PROVISIONS

## 1. Definitions and interpretation

### 1.1 Definitions

In this Lease, unless the contrary intention appears:

**Act** means the Retail Leases Act 2003 (Vic).

**Base rent** means the amount in Item 14 as reviewed under clause 4.

**Building** means the building and land in Item 4, including the common areas, and any part of it.

**Business day** means a day that is not a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in Bendigo.

**Common areas** means those parts of the Building not let or intended to be let to a tenant, including the rear yard, the Bath Lane loading area, the bin enclosure, the shared switchboard cupboard and the shared water meter cupboard.

**Contaminant** means any substance which, when present in or on land, groundwater, air or a building, gives rise to or is capable of giving rise to harm to human health or the environment, and includes perchloroethylene, hydrocarbon solvent, solvent residues, still bottoms, spent filter cartridges, waste water and separator water.

**Environment Protection Law** means the Environment Protection Act 2017 (Vic), the Environment Protection Regulations 2021 (Vic), any environment reference standard, any compliance code or guideline published by the Environment Protection Authority Victoria, and any successor legislation.

**GST** has the meaning given in the A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) Act 1999 (Cth).

**Landlord's fixtures** means the items listed in Annexure C and any item the Landlord installs in the Premises during the Term at the Landlord's cost.

**Outgoings** has the meaning given in section 3 of the Act and, for this Lease, is limited to the items listed in clause 5.2.

**Premises** means the premises in Item 5.

**Rent** means base rent and every other amount payable by the Tenant under this Lease.

**Tenant's plant** means the dry cleaning machines, boiler, compressor, presses, finishing equipment, conveyor, still, solvent store, tanks, pumps, ducting and associated services installed in the Premises by the Tenant or by a previous occupier and acquired by the Tenant, and includes the items listed in Annexure D.

**Term** means the term in Item 11 and, where the option in clause 3.3 is validly exercised, includes the further term.

### 1.2 Interpretation

(a) A reference to legislation includes that legislation as amended or replaced.

(b) The singular includes the plural and the reverse.

(c) A reference to a party includes that party's successors and permitted assigns.

(d) Where two or more persons are named as Tenant, they are bound jointly and severally.

(e) Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

(f) A reference to a clause, Item or Annexure is a reference to a clause of this Lease, an Item of the Reference Schedule or an Annexure to this Lease.

(g) Money is Australian currency and measurements are metric.

(h) Where a thing is to be done on a day that is not a business day, it must be done on the next business day.

### 1.3 The Act prevails

If any provision of this Lease is inconsistent with the Act, the Act prevails and the provision is read down to the extent of the inconsistency. If a provision cannot be read down it is severed and the rest of this Lease continues in force.

## 2. Grant

### 2.1 Grant of lease

The Landlord leases the Premises to the Tenant for the Term at the Rent and on the terms of this Lease.

### 2.2 Exclusive possession

The Tenant has exclusive possession of the Premises, subject to the Landlord's rights of entry under clause 9.5.

### 2.3 Rights granted

The Tenant has, in common with the Landlord and others authorised by the Landlord:

(a) the right to use the common areas for pedestrian and vehicular access;

(b) the right to use the rear loading area off Bath Lane between 6:00 am and 7:00 pm on any day for deliveries, for the delivery and collection of solvent, and for the collection of waste; and

(c) the right to run and maintain the services which serve the Premises through the Building, including the boiler flue, the solvent vent stack and the trade waste line.

### 2.4 Exceptions

The Tenant has no right to the roof, the external walls, the airspace above the Building or any part of the Building other than the Premises, except as clause 2.3 and clause 6.6 allow.

## 3. Term and option to renew

### 3.1 Term

The Term commences on the date in Item 9 and, unless ended earlier under this Lease, ends on the date in Item 10.

### 3.2 Landlord's notice of the option

The Landlord must, in accordance with section 28 of the Act, notify the Tenant in writing of the date after which the option in clause 3.3 is no longer exercisable. That notice must be given at least 6 months and not more than 12 months before the last day of the option exercise period in Item 13. If the Landlord gives that notice late, the last day for exercise is extended in accordance with section 28(2) of the Act.

### 3.3 Option

If the Tenant gives the Landlord written notice within the option exercise period in Item 13, the Landlord must grant the Tenant a lease of the Premises for the further term in Item 12 on the same terms as this Lease except that:

(a) there is no further option to renew;

(b) the base rent for the first year of the further term is the base rent payable in the final year of the Term, reviewed to the Consumer Price Index under clause 4.2 on 1 March 2029; and

(c) the security in clause 13 is adjusted to three months' base rent plus GST as at the commencement of the further term.

### 3.4 Preconditions to option

The Tenant may exercise the option only if, at the date of the notice and at the date the further term begins:

(a) the Tenant has remedied any breach of this Lease of which the Landlord has given written notice; and

(b) the Tenant has not, on three or more occasions in the preceding 24 months, been given written notice of a failure to pay Rent within 14 days of its due date and failed to remedy that failure within a further 14 days.

The Landlord may waive either precondition. A breach that has been remedied does not prevent exercise of the option.

### 3.5 Holding over

If the Tenant remains in possession after the Terminating date with the Landlord's consent and without a further lease being granted, the Tenant holds over as a monthly tenant on the terms of this Lease so far as they can apply to a monthly tenancy, at the base rent payable immediately before the Terminating date, terminable by either party on one month's written notice expiring on any day.

## 4. Rent and rent review

### 4.1 Payment of base rent

The Tenant must pay the base rent to the Landlord by equal monthly instalments of $4,866.67 plus GST in advance on the first day of each month, without deduction, set-off or demand, by electronic funds transfer to the account nominated by the Landlord from time to time. The first instalment was payable on 1 March 2024. Where a period is less than a full month, the instalment is apportioned on a daily basis.

### 4.2 CPI review

On each date in Item 15 the base rent is adjusted to the amount calculated as:

R × (A ÷ B)

where:

R is the base rent payable immediately before the review date;

A is the Consumer Price Index (All Groups, Melbourne) published by the Australian Bureau of Statistics for the last quarter for which the index was published before the review date; and

B is the same index for the quarter 12 months before the quarter used for A.

### 4.3 Review machinery

(a) The Landlord must give the Tenant written notice of the reviewed base rent, setting out the index numbers used and the calculation.

(b) Until that notice is given, the Tenant continues to pay the base rent applying before the review date. Within 14 days after the notice, the Tenant must pay the shortfall for the period since the review date.

(c) If the index is discontinued or its base is changed, the index is replaced or converted in accordance with section 36 of the Act and, failing agreement, as determined by a specialist retail valuer appointed under Part 5 of the Act.

(d) A review takes effect on the review date whether or not the notice under paragraph (a) is given on time.

### 4.4 No ratchet

If the calculation in clause 4.2 produces an amount less than the base rent payable before the review date, the base rent is reduced to that lesser amount. Any provision of this Lease which would prevent the base rent decreasing on review is void under section 35(2) of the Act.

### 4.5 Interest on late payment

If the Tenant does not pay any Rent within 14 days after its due date, the Tenant must pay interest on the unpaid amount from the due date until payment at the rate fixed from time to time under section 2 of the Penalty Interest Rates Act 1983 (Vic). The Landlord's right to interest does not affect any other right the Landlord has.

## 5. Outgoings

### 5.1 Tenant to pay

The Tenant must pay to the Landlord the Tenant's proportion in Item 8 of the outgoings in clause 5.2, in monthly instalments in advance on the days the base rent is payable, based on the estimate given under clause 5.3 and adjusted under clause 5.5.

### 5.2 Recoverable outgoings

The only outgoings recoverable under this Lease are:

(a) council rates and charges;

(b) water rates and charges and the fixed component of the trade waste agreement for the Building, other than volumetric and load-based trade waste charges which the Tenant pays direct under clause 5.7;

(c) the fire services property levy;

(d) insurance premiums for the Building under clause 9.1 and the reasonable cost of a valuation for insurance purposes, not more often than once every three years;

(e) the cost of cleaning, lighting, repairing and maintaining the common areas, including the bin enclosure and the Bath Lane loading area;

(f) the cost of the annual test and inspection of the fire services and essential safety measures for the Building;

(g) the cost of pest control for the common areas;

(h) waste collection for the common areas; and

(i) the management fee charged by the Landlord's managing agent, not exceeding 4% of the gross rental income of the Building.

### 5.3 Estimate

The Landlord must give the Tenant a written estimate of the outgoings for each accounting period, itemised by the categories in clause 5.2, at least one month before the start of that accounting period and, for the first accounting period, before the Commencing date. If the Landlord does not give the estimate, the Tenant is not required to pay the outgoings until the estimate is given, in accordance with section 46(3) of the Act.

### 5.4 Statement

Within three months after the end of each accounting period the Landlord must give the Tenant a written statement of the outgoings actually incurred, itemised by the categories in clause 5.2, prepared and reported on in accordance with sections 46 and 47 of the Act. The statement must be accompanied by an auditor's report unless the Act does not require one.

### 5.5 Reconciliation

If the statement shows that the Tenant has paid more than the Tenant's proportion of the outgoings actually incurred, the Landlord must refund the excess within 28 days or, at the Tenant's election, credit it against the next instalments of Rent. If the statement shows the Tenant has paid less, the Tenant must pay the difference within 28 days after receiving the statement, but only to the extent permitted by section 47 of the Act.

### 5.6 Prohibited amounts

The Tenant is not liable for and must not be charged:

(a) land tax, or any amount on account of land tax, which is not recoverable by reason of section 50 of the Act;

(b) any capital cost of the Building or any contribution to a sinking fund;

(c) any cost of the Landlord's own management other than the fee in clause 5.2(i); or

(d) any outgoing which is not listed in clause 5.2 or which is not stated in the disclosure statement.

### 5.7 Tenant's own charges

The Tenant must pay direct to the supplier all charges for electricity, gas, telephone and internet supplied to the Premises and separately metered, and all volumetric and load-based trade waste charges under the Tenant's own trade waste agreement with the water authority.

## 6. Tenant's covenants

### 6.1 Use

The Tenant must use the Premises only for the permitted use in Item 18. The Tenant must not use the Premises for a public auction, as a residence, or for the storage of any goods not required for the permitted use.

### 6.2 Continuous trading

The Tenant must keep the retail part of the Premises open for business during the core trading hours in Item 19, properly stocked and staffed, unless prevented by an event beyond the Tenant's control. The Tenant may trade outside the core trading hours. Nothing in this clause requires the Tenant to trade on a Sunday or a public holiday.

### 6.3 Repair and maintenance

The Tenant must keep the Premises in good and substantial repair and condition, fair wear and tear and damage covered by the Landlord's insurance excepted. This obligation includes:

(a) the shop front, entry doors, door furniture and rear roller door;

(b) the internal walls, floor coverings, ceilings, ceiling grid and light fittings;

(c) the plumbing, drainage and electrical services exclusively serving the Premises, from the point of connection to the Building services;

(d) the grease and lint traps, the trade waste pre-treatment pit and the solvent separator; and

(e) the exhaust ducting, make-up air louvres and the boiler flue from the boiler to the point of discharge.

### 6.4 Servicing of plant

The Tenant must maintain, under written service agreements with suitably qualified contractors, the air conditioning serving the Premises (at least twice each year), the exhaust and make-up air systems (at least twice each year) and the grease and lint traps (at least quarterly), and must give the Landlord copies of the service records within 14 days of a written request.

### 6.5 Alterations

The Tenant must not alter the Premises without the Landlord's prior written consent, which must not be unreasonably withheld for an alteration that does not affect the structure, the roof, the external appearance or the Building services. The Tenant must give the Landlord plans and specifications, and must carry out approved works in a proper and workmanlike manner, in compliance with all laws, with all necessary building and planning approvals obtained at the Tenant's cost.

### 6.6 Signage

The Tenant may keep and maintain the fascia sign above the shop front and one under-awning sign, in each case to the dimensions and position approved by the Landlord in writing before the Commencing date. The Tenant must keep the signs clean, safe and properly illuminated, must hold and comply with any planning permit for them, and must remove them and make good at the end of the Term. The Tenant must not affix any other sign to the exterior of the Building.

### 6.7 Cleanliness

The Tenant must keep the Premises clean and free of rubbish, must not allow waste to accumulate in the common areas, must keep the bin enclosure tidy and the lids closed, and must remove all trade waste from the Building at the Tenant's cost.

### 6.8 No nuisance

The Tenant must not cause or allow any nuisance, and must take all practicable steps to prevent noise, odour, vibration, steam, heat or fumes from the Premises interfering with the occupiers of the Building or of neighbouring land. Without limiting that obligation, the Tenant must operate the solvent machines with the doors closed, must keep the carbon adsorber in service, and must not vent solvent-laden air other than through the approved stack.

### 6.9 Compliance with laws

The Tenant must comply, at the Tenant's cost, with every law, regulation, permit, licence, registration and notice applying to the Tenant's use or occupation of the Premises, including the Occupational Health and Safety Act 2004 (Vic), the Occupational Health and Safety Regulations 2017 (Vic), the Dangerous Goods Act 1985 (Vic) and the Environment Protection Law. The Tenant must give the Landlord a copy of any notice, direction or order received from a regulator in relation to the Premises within 3 business days of receiving it.

### 6.10 Security of the Premises

The Tenant must keep the Premises securely locked when unattended, must maintain the locks, alarm and external security lighting, and must give the Landlord a set of keys and current alarm contact details for use in an emergency.

## 7. Environmental and plant obligations

### 7.1 Solvent storage and bunding

The Tenant must store all solvent, waste solvent and solvent residue in the bunded solvent store at the rear of the plant area. The Tenant must ensure that:

(a) the bund is impervious, is kept free of stored water and is capable of retaining at least 110% of the volume of the largest container stored in it, being not less than 220 L;

(b) no more than two 200 L drums of unused solvent and two 200 L drums of waste residue are held in the Premises at any time;

(c) drums are kept closed, labelled and on a hard, sealed, chemically resistant surface which is not connected to any stormwater drain;

(d) the manifest and the safety data sheets are kept at the Premises and are available at the Bath Lane entry point; and

(e) the store is kept locked when the Premises are unattended.

### 7.2 Spill containment

The Tenant must keep a fully stocked solvent spill kit adjacent to the solvent store and a second kit adjacent to the dry cleaning machines. The Tenant must clean up any spill immediately, must not allow any Contaminant to enter the stormwater system, the sewer or the ground, and must notify the Landlord in writing within 24 hours of any spill of more than 5 L or of any spill of any volume which reaches soil or a drain.

### 7.3 EPA obligations

The Tenant is the person in management or control of the Premises for the purposes of the Environment Protection Law and holds the general environmental duty in respect of the Tenant's activities. The Tenant must:

(a) identify and minimise the risks of harm to human health and the environment from its use of solvents, so far as reasonably practicable;

(b) transport and dispose of all prescribed industrial waste only through a person authorised by the Environment Protection Authority Victoria to receive and transport it, and keep the waste transport certificates for at least five years;

(c) not cause or permit a notifiable contamination event to occur and, if one occurs, notify the Authority as required and give the Landlord a copy of that notification on the same day; and

(d) comply with any notice issued under the Environment Protection Law affecting the Premises at the Tenant's cost, whether the notice is directed to the Tenant or to the Landlord, where the notice arises from the Tenant's use or occupation.

### 7.4 Contamination indemnity

The Tenant indemnifies the Landlord against all liability, loss, cost and expense (including the cost of assessment, clean-up, remediation, monitoring and legal costs) arising from the presence, escape or migration of any Contaminant in, on, under or from the Premises to the extent caused or contributed to by the Tenant's use or occupation of the Premises during the Term or during any earlier period of occupation by the Tenant. This indemnity does not extend to contamination shown by the report in Item 27 to have existed before the Commencing date, except to the extent the Tenant's use makes that contamination worse. This clause survives the end of this Lease.

### 7.5 Boiler and pressure vessels

(a) The Tenant must keep the vertical gas-fired steam boiler installed in the plant room, registration VIC-PV-118-4470, and the air receiver serving the compressor, registered, inspected and maintained in accordance with AS 3788 and the Occupational Health and Safety Regulations 2017 (Vic).

(b) The Tenant must arrange an annual external inspection of the boiler by a competent person and must give the Landlord a copy of each inspection report and of the current registration within 14 days of a written request.

(c) The Tenant must not allow the registration to lapse while the boiler remains in the Premises, must keep the boiler log at the Premises, and must notify the Landlord within 3 business days if the boiler is taken out of service for more than 14 days.

### 7.6 Trade waste

The Tenant must hold and comply with a trade waste agreement with the water authority for the discharge from the Premises, must maintain the solvent separator and pre-treatment pit, must have the separator emptied and the sludge removed as prescribed industrial waste at least every six months, and must keep the discharge within the limits of that agreement. The Tenant must not discharge any solvent, separator water or still bottoms to sewer or stormwater, and must keep the disposal records for at least five years.

### 7.7 Exhaust and ventilation

The Tenant must operate and maintain the exhaust and ventilation systems serving the plant area, including the carbon adsorber and the solvent vent stack, so that the workplace exposure standard for perchloroethylene is not exceeded. The Tenant must have the plant area atmosphere monitored at least once each year by a competent person and must give the Landlord a copy of the result on written request. The stack must not be altered, extended or relocated without the Landlord's prior written consent.

### 7.8 Environmental site condition reports

(a) The parties acknowledge that the site condition report in Item 27, prepared by Terrafield Environmental Pty Ltd and annexed as Annexure B, was obtained jointly before the Commencing date. It records the condition of the Premises, the soil and the groundwater at the Commencing date and is the baseline against which the Tenant's obligations under clauses 7.4 and 14.6 are measured.

(b) Not earlier than three months before and not later than the last day of the Term, the Tenant must obtain and give to the Landlord a further site condition report for the Premises, prepared at the Tenant's cost by an environmental consultant with appropriate professional indemnity insurance, reasonably acceptable to the Landlord, and using a scope and sampling locations equivalent to those in Annexure B.

(c) If the exit report shows contamination which is worse than the baseline in Annexure B and which is attributable to the Tenant's use, the Tenant must, at the Tenant's cost, carry out the remediation reasonably required to return the Premises to the baseline condition, and clause 14.6 applies to that work.

### 7.9 Plant penetrations

The Tenant must keep every penetration of the floor slab, walls, roof and ceiling made for the Tenant's plant properly sealed, flashed and weathertight, and must keep a written record of each penetration showing its location and purpose. That record must be given to the Landlord with the exit report under clause 7.8(b).

The Tenant occupies the Premises at its own risk and releases the Landlord from liability for loss or damage to the Tenant's property or business caused by the escape of water, steam, gas or electricity, or by the act of any other occupier, except to the extent caused by the negligence or default of the Landlord.

### 9.5 Landlord's right of entry

The Landlord and its agents may enter the Premises at any reasonable time on not less than 48 hours' written notice, or at any time in an emergency without notice, to inspect, to carry out the Landlord's obligations, to carry out work required by a public authority, and to show the Premises to a prospective purchaser or, during the last six months of the Term, to a prospective tenant. Entry to the plant area is subject to the Tenant's site induction and to the Tenant's reasonable safety directions.

## 10. Damage and destruction

### 10.1 Abatement

If the Premises or the Building are damaged so that the Premises or any part of them are unfit for use or inaccessible, the base rent and outgoings abate from the date of damage in proportion to the part of the Premises unfit for use or inaccessible, until the Premises are again fit for use and accessible.

### 10.2 Repair or termination

(a) The Landlord is not obliged to reinstate the Premises or the Building.

(b) If the Landlord notifies the Tenant that the Landlord does not intend to reinstate, either party may terminate this Lease by 7 days' written notice.

(c) If the Landlord does not repair the damage within a reasonable time after the Tenant asks in writing, the Tenant may terminate this Lease by 7 days' written notice.

(d) This clause operates subject to and in accordance with section 57 of the Act.

### 10.3 Tenant's fault

Clauses 10.1 and 10.2 do not apply if the damage is caused or contributed to by the Tenant, or by an act or omission of the Tenant's employees, contractors or customers, or by the Tenant's plant, and to that extent the Rent does not abate.

## 11. Assignment and subletting

### 11.1 Consent required

The Tenant must not assign, sublet, part with possession of, mortgage or otherwise deal with its interest in the Premises without the Landlord's prior written consent.

### 11.2 Grounds for withholding consent

The Landlord may withhold consent to an assignment only on a ground permitted by section 60 of the Act, including where the proposed assignee proposes to change the use of the Premises, where the proposed assignee has financial resources or retailing skills inferior to those of the Tenant, or where the Tenant has not complied with clause 11.3.

### 11.3 Process

The Tenant must:

(a) ask for consent in writing and give the Landlord information the Landlord reasonably requires about the proposed assignee's financial standing and retailing skills;

(b) give the proposed assignee a copy of the disclosure statement given to the Tenant, updated in accordance with section 61 of the Act, together with details of any change in the information in it, at least 7 days before asking for consent; and

(c) procure that the proposed assignee gives the Landlord a written statement that it has received and read that disclosure statement.

### 11.4 Landlord's response

The Landlord must deal with the request in accordance with section 60 of the Act. If the Landlord has not given or withheld consent within 28 days after the request and all required information are given, the Landlord is taken to have consented.

### 11.5 Conditions of consent
