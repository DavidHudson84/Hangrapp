Structured summary of the Main Street Dry Cleaners retail price list for 2026 — the posted counter price list for both the Bendigo store and the Kangaroo Flat drop store.

Effective 1 July 2026; supersedes the 1 July 2025 list. All prices include GST. Prices are per item unless the line says otherwise and are identical at both sites. Standard turnaround at Bendigo is next business day for dry cleaning lodged before 9:00 am, same day for shirts lodged before 9:00 am Mon–Fri.

Headline counter prices. Two-piece suit $29.50 (men's or women's, pieces lodged together). Three-piece suit $38.00. Jacket or blazer $17.50. Trousers $14.50. Laundered business shirt on a hanger $6.50, folded $7.00, boxed $7.50; five or more lodged together $5.90 each on a hanger. Day dress $21.50, cocktail $29.50, evening or full length $38.00, beaded from $58.00. Coat three-quarter $23.50, full length $28.00.

Wash, dry and fold is per kilo, weighed dry at lodgement: $7.50/kg sorted by the customer, $8.50/kg unsorted, minimum charge $30.00 (equals 4 kg sorted), delicates surcharge $4.00/kg, two business days.

Manchester: doonas are priced by size then a fill loading added. Single $42.00, double $48.00, queen $54.00, king $59.00, super king $66.00; synthetic fill included, wool +$6.00, down or feather +$14.00, silk +$18.00 — so a queen wool doona is $60.00 and a queen down doona is $68.00.

Curtains are priced by the drop (one panel; a pair is two drops), in 500 mm bands. Unlined runs $28.00 under 1.5 m up to from $56.00 at 3.0 m and over; lined $36.00 to from $72.00; blockout $42.00 to from $86.00; sheers $22.00 to from $45.00. Take-down and re-hang is $88.00 per hour, one operator, same minimum, by appointment Tuesday and Thursday. Relaxation shrinkage of 3–5% (up to 10% on loose-weave or sun-damaged fabric) is normal, is not preventable and is not accepted as a claim.

Wedding: gown clean from $210.00 plain, from $280.00 beaded or with a train; clean and preserve in an acid-free box from $340.00; box supplied alone $135.00. Veil $48.00 short, $68.00 cathedral. Bridesmaid dress $42.00. Page boy suit $24.00. 10–14 business days, no express.

Quoted rather than listed: all leather, suede and sheepskin (goes to a Melbourne specialist, three to four weeks, quoted on inspection — leather jacket from $145.00 is only a starting figure); at-risk garments; and commercial linen and workwear contract rates (contract pricing starts below list at 200 pieces a week).

Surcharges and express: same-day express list plus 50%, minimum $12.00, Bendigo only, in before 9:00 am; next-day express on 2–3 day items list plus 30%, minimum $8.00; Saturday express list plus 60%, minimum $15.00. Minimum counter order $12.00. Delivery Zone 1 free over $45.00 spend ($9.50 under), Zone 2 $12.00 with $60.00 minimum, Zone 3 $18.00 with $90.00 minimum. Storage free 30 days then $2.00 per garment per week; uncollected at 90 days. Free re-clean within 7 days with the docket.

Agency: Kangaroo Flat charges the same prices with no loading, but turnaround is 48 hours rather than 24, shirts included, and no same-day express. Curtains, gowns, leather and alterations are quoted and finished at Bendigo.

Accounts: 30 days from the monthly statement, stop at 60 days. Discount on previous month's spend ex GST — 5% at $500, 10% at $1,500, 12.5% at $4,000 and over. EFTPOS, Visa, Mastercard, cash, EFT; 1.1% credit card surcharge. Claims go to the Fair Claims Guide.
