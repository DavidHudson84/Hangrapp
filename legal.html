Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026

Hangr Consultant ("Hangr", "we", "us") is an AI advisor for Australian dry cleaning operators. This policy explains what personal information we handle, why, and your rights under the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). Questions: david@hudsongroup.com.au.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email, business name and login credentials.
  • Your business profile: sites, staff numbers, equipment, solvents, suppliers, ABN and logo.
  • What you put into the app: chats, complaint and claim details, HR matters, documents you upload, photos you attach, and the letters and notes you generate. Some of this may include personal information about other people — your customers, staff or suppliers — that you choose to enter.
  • Usage information: basic records of how and when the app is used, to run and improve the service.

We collect this directly from you as you use the app. We don't buy it from anyone or collect it covertly.

Why we collect it

To provide the service: to give you profile-aware advice, generate your letters and documents, store your work so it's there next time, and keep the app secure. We use your information for the purpose you gave it to us for, and for directly related purposes you would reasonably expect.

How your information is used with AI

Hangr works by sending your question, along with the relevant parts of your profile and any document you're asking about, to our AI provider (Anthropic), which generates the response. Anthropic processes it outside Australia, in the United States. Under Anthropic's commercial API terms, your inputs and the outputs are not used to train their AI models.

By using Hangr you consent to this overseas processing for the purpose of generating your answers (APP 8). If there is particular personal information you don't want sent to the AI, don't enter it into a chat.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information or share it for marketing. We use a small number of service providers to run the app: Anthropic (generates the AI responses; United States) and Supabase (secure database and hosting; your data is stored in Australia, Sydney region). These providers handle your data only to run the service, under their own security and privacy obligations. We may also disclose information where required by law.

Where your data is stored and how it's protected

Your data is stored in Australia (Sydney). It is encrypted in transit and at rest, walled off so one business can never see another's, and access is controlled by login and role. Our AI key and your data are held on the server, never in your browser.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your data while your account is active. You can ask us to export or delete your data at any time — email david@hudsongroup.com.au and we'll action it. When you close your account we remove your data from the live system; backup copies are cycled out on our normal backup schedule.

Accessing and correcting your information (APP 12 & 13)

You can see and correct most of your information directly in the app — your profile, chats and letters. For anything else, contact us and we'll give you access or make the correction, as the APPs require.

Complaints

If you think we've mishandled your information, email us first at david@hudsongroup.com.au and we'll work to resolve it. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this policy as the service grows, and post the current version here with its date.

How your data is handled

The short, plain-English version of where your information goes and how it's kept. The Privacy Policy has the full detail.

It's your data, and it stays yours

Everything you put into Hangr — your profile, chats, claims, letters and documents — belongs to you. You can export it or ask us to delete it whenever you want.

It's stored in Australia

Your data lives in a secure database hosted in Australia (Sydney). It's encrypted on the way there and while it's stored.

It's walled off from other businesses

Every operator's data sits in its own space. One business can never see another's chats, claims or documents. This is enforced by the system, not just hidden on the screen.

Who in your business sees what

  • Owner — sees everything and manages who has access.
  • Admin — sees and uploads everything.
  • Staff — chat and the letters they create, not your claims, financials or documents.

Your login is protected

Accounts are protected by a password and two-factor authentication — a second code at login — so a password on its own isn't enough to get in.

How the AI part works

When you ask a question, Hangr sends it, along with the relevant parts of your profile and any document you're asking about, to a secure AI provider (Anthropic) to generate the answer. That provider processes it overseas (in the US) and, under its terms, does not use your content to train its AI. If there's something you'd rather the AI never see, don't type it into a chat.

Backups

Your data is backed up so it isn't trapped on one machine or lost if something fails. You can also export your own copy at any time.

It's a pilot

Hangr is being trialled with a small group of operators. Please tell us what's wrong, missing or unclear — that's the whole point of the pilot.
